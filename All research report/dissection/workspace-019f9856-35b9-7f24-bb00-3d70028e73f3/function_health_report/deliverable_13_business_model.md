## 13.4 LIFETIME VALUE (LTV) & RETENTION

### Confirmed / Strong Inference 🟢 / 🟡

**LTV Factors:**
- **Annual Membership:** $365 base
- **Add-On Spending:** Average member likely spends $500-$1,000+ per year (MRI $499, retests $269, extended panels, concierge draws)
- **Retention:** Annual model requires renewal; user complaints suggest retention challenges (scheduling friction, AI note quality, upsell fatigue)
- **Estimated LTV:** $365-$1,000+ per year × estimated retention years (likely 1-3 years based on user review patterns)

**Retention Challenges:**
- Scheduling errors and manual customer service requirements
- Batch result delivery delays
- AI note quality dissatisfaction
- Incorrect biomarker range interpretations (potential liability)
- Hidden retest costs causing price shock
- Upsell fatigue
- No mobile app for easy engagement
- Competitor alternatives (Mito Health, Superpower) mentioned in user reviews

## 13.5 MARGINS & UNIT ECONOMICS

### Strong Inference 🟡

**Cost Structure (Estimated):**
- **Lab Partner Costs (Quest):** Significant portion of membership fee; Quest captures economics per test; estimated 40-60% of $365 flows to lab partners
- **Clinician Review Costs:** Human clinician review of every result; at scale (100K+ members, 160 markers × 2 visits = 32M results per year), clinician costs are substantial
- **AI Inference Costs:** LLM inference for Private AI Chat, Protocols, health record processing; costs scale with usage
- **Technology & Infrastructure:** Google Cloud Run, Firebase, database storage, monitoring; estimated 10-15% of revenue
- **Marketing & Sales:** Celebrity partnerships, influencer marketing, paid advertising; estimated 20-30% of revenue
- **Operations:** Customer support (offshore), scheduling management, quality assurance, regulatory compliance; estimated 10-15% of revenue

**Estimated Gross Margin:** 40-60% (before clinician and AI costs)
**Estimated Net Margin:** Potentially 10-25% at scale; lower during growth phase due to high CAC and technology investment

# PORTER'S FIVE FORCES — FUNCTION HEALTH

FORCE 1: THREAT OF NEW ENTRANTS — MEDIUM
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evidence:
- Barriers: Lab partner integration (Quest), clinical advisory board, celebrity branding, funding ($351M), AI technology, regulatory compliance (HIPAA, state lab regulations)
- Low barriers: Web technology, direct-to-consumer model, open-source AI, lab testing accessibility (Quest serves many competitors)
- New entrants: Mito Health, Superpower, Regacore, Levels, PreventiveHealth.ai, Everlywell, LetsGetChecked, Base
- Evidence: Tracxn lists 51 active competitors including 10 funded
- Inference: Medium threat — barriers exist but are not insurmountable; technology and lab access are widely available; celebrity branding is replicable with sufficient funding

FORCE 2: BARGAINING POWER OF SUPPLIERS — MEDIUM TO HIGH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evidence:
- Lab partner: Quest Diagnostics (2,000+ locations) — significant supplier; Quest captures significant economics per test; Function depends on Quest for testing infrastructure
- Imaging partner/acquisition: Ezra (owned) — reduces supplier power for imaging; but MRI/CT equipment and AI analysis require specialized technology
- Supplement database: SuppCo (owned) — reduces supplier power for supplement intelligence
- Cloud provider: Google Cloud — significant infrastructure supplier; but alternatives exist (AWS, Azure)
- AI model provider: Unknown — if OpenAI/Anthropic, supplier power is high; if custom/fine-tuned, supplier power is lower
- Evidence: User reports mention Quest website access blocked; scheduling must go through Quest; results delivered in batches by Quest
- Inference: Medium to high supplier power — Quest is a critical supplier with significant negotiating leverage; but Function's scale (50M+ tests) provides some bargaining power

FORCE 3: BARGAINING POWER OF BUYERS — MEDIUM
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evidence:
- Consumer price sensitivity: Price reduced from $499 to $365; user complaints about hidden retest costs ($269); competitor alternatives (Mito Health, Superpower, Everlywell, LetsGetChecked)
- Annual subscription: Members must renew; switching costs exist (health record uploads, biomarker history) but are not prohibitive
- No exclusive contracts: Members can cancel and switch to competitors; data is portable
- Evidence: User reviews confirm competitors mentioned; some users quit Function and plan to order tests directly from Quest; price sensitivity evident
- Inference: Medium buyer power — consumers have alternatives; price sensitivity exists; but health data investment and clinical review provide some retention

FORCE 4: THREAT OF SUBSTITUTES — HIGH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evidence:
- Direct substitutes: Mito Health, Superpower, Regacore, Levels, PreventiveHealth.ai, LetsGetChecked, Everlywell, Base — all offer similar or overlapping services
- Indirect substitutes: Standard annual physical (26 biomarkers — less depth but covered by insurance); direct Quest/LabCorp ordering (lower cost, no AI interpretation); wearable health tracking (Oura, Whoop — continuous but less clinical depth); telemedicine (Teladoc, Amwell — acute care, not preventive); health coaching (1-on-1 coaching — more personalized but more expensive)
- Technology substitutes: ChatGPT/Gemini health analysis (users import Function results into AI for additional analysis); open-source health AI (potential future disruption)
- Evidence: User reviews mention using ChatGPT/Gemini for analysis; competitor comparisons frequent; standard physical mentioned as alternative
- Inference: High threat of substitutes — many alternatives exist at various price points and service levels; technology (AI, wearables) enables new substitutes rapidly

FORCE 5: RIVALRY AMONG EXISTING COMPETITORS — HIGH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Evidence:
- Direct competitors: Mito Health, Superpower, Regacore, Levels, PreventiveHealth.ai, Everlywell, LetsGetChecked, Base — 51 total competitors (10 funded)
- Similar business models: Direct-to-consumer biomarker testing; subscription pricing; AI interpretation; clinical review (variable)
- Price competition: Function reduced price from $499 to $365; competitor pricing likely competitive; add-on pricing ($499 MRI, $269 retest) creates price comparison points
- Differentiation: Function's 160+ biomarkers, clinical board, celebrity branding, imaging integration, supplement intelligence — but competitors rapidly copying features
- Growth rate: Preventive health market growing (10-15% estimated); attracts new entrants and investment
- Evidence: Tracxn competitor list; user reviews comparing Function to Mito Health; news coverage of competitor funding; market growth indicators
- Inference: High rivalry — many competitors; rapid feature copying; price competition; market growth attracts investment; differentiation requires continuous innovation (AI, imaging, supplements, clinical validation, international, mobile, developer ecosystem)

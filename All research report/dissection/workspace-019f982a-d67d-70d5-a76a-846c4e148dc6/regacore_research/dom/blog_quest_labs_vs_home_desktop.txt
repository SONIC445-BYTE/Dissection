Quest Labs vs. At-Home Draws: The Phlebotomy Experience
June 05, 2026
4 min read
|
Written by Regacore Medical Advisory Board

How we streamlined the blood testing experience, from 2,000+ Quest locations to clinical phlebotomy at your door.

Getting advanced blood work should not be a hassle. We designed the Regacore phlebotomy process to fit seamlessly into a busy schedule.

1. Standard Partner Lab Visits

Through our partnership with Quest Diagnostics, members can visit any of 2,000+ clinical laboratories nationwide. Testing is pre-approved and takes under 15 minutes with no billing paperwork at checkout.

2. Premium At-Home Collection

For ultimate convenience, select the mobile blood draw option. A certified mobile phlebotomist will visit your home or office. Samples are safely collected and immediately packed in temperature-controlled transport to preserve fragile biomarkers.

3. Integrated Results Dashboard

Once processed, your data flows directly into your digital twin. You will receive an alert to review your results with our medical team, with no need to coordinate lab paperwork.

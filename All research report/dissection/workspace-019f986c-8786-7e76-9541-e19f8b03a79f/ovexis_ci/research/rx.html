FAQs

Find answers to your most pressing questions about HealthifyRx and its benefits.

Who is this program meant for?

HealthifyRx is designed for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27+ with health conditions like diabetes, prediabetes, PCOS or hypertension. A doctor will assess your medical history to confirm eligibility.

What medication do you use?

We currently use either Semaglutide like Wegovy or Tirzepatide like Yurpeak, Mounjaro, both cutting-edge GLP-1 medications shown to deliver up to 20%* weight loss. The medication is prescribed only after a full medical evaluation and supervision.

Are there side effects? Is it safe?

Most users experience mild side effects like nausea, constipation, or fatigue in the early weeks. We minimize these with dose titration, meal timing adjustments, and optional doctor-cleared relief kits. All side effects are tracked and addressed within 12 hours.

Our medical team also screens for any underlying health risks before you begin. If you’re not a fit for GLP-1s due to certain medical conditions (like pancreatitis, thyroid cancer risk, or severe GI disease), we won’t proceed with the prescription.

Your safety comes first — always.

Do I have to take the medication forever?

Many people reduce or even stop medication after reaching their goal. We support this with a transition and maintenance plan designed to lock in results and prevent relapse.

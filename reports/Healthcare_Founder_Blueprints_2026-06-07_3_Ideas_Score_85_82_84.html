1) AutoAppeal AI: Autonomous Prior Auth Clearinghouse — Score: 85/100

ID: 1a2b3c4d-5e6f-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d

TL;DR: An autonomous AI clearinghouse that ingests EHR data to auto-generate and win prior authorization appeals, unlocking a $45B administrative TAM by fighting payer friction.

Evidence

  • 70% of commercially insured patients were initially denied coverage for at least one newly prescribed brand medicine, forcing delayed treatments. — [source]
  • Patients are forced to delay major life events, like weddings, simply to avoid transferring crippling medical debt and insurance liabilities to partners. — [source]
  • Institutional denials at public clinics force patients into the expensive private market, exacerbated by national medicine shortages. — [source]

Problem

Profit-driven payer models use bureaucratic friction as a cost-containment strategy, leading to a 13 percentage point increase in claim rejections between 2021 and 2025. This 'deny first, fix later' approach results in 25% of new prescriptions remaining denied after a year, causing serious adverse events for patients and massive uncompensated care for hospitals.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Regulatory capture by Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) and massive insurance conglomerates prevents meaningful legislative reform.
  • Fragmented interoperability between provider Electronic Health Records (EHRs) and payer systems ensures the appeals process remains highly manual and opaque.
  • Providers lack the human administrative stamina to fight every denial, making payer friction an effective economic barrier.

AI-First Solution

A specialized RAG-based LLM system that integrates directly with hospital EHRs to cross-reference patient clinical histories against payer-specific medical necessity guidelines, automatically drafting and submitting comprehensive appeal packets.

  • Core: Federated learning across hospital networks to identify and predict successful appeal patterns against specific payer algorithms.
  • Automated Appeal Generation — Outputting medically coded, evidence-backed appeal letters with a measured first-pass win rate metric.
  • Bi-directional EHR Integration — Pulling clinical notes via Epic/Cerner FHIR APIs and pushing status updates back to the revenue cycle dashboard.
  • Payer Policy Tracker — A continuous feedback loop that updates the vector database when payers silently change their algorithmic denial criteria.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: First-pass appeal overturn rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital systems integrated
  • Economic KPI: Gross margin per automated appeal

2) FlowState: Predictive Triage & Load Balancer — Score: 82/100

ID: 2b3c4d5e-6f7a-8b9c-0d1e-2f3a4b5c6d7e

TL;DR: A predictive patient flow engine that ingests real-time hospital data to dynamically route non-critical patients, solving the $85B capacity crisis causing deadly wait times.

Evidence

  • In early 2026, UK A&E staff experienced a record high of over 2.3 million attendances, leaving patients waiting hours for emergency care. — [source]
  • In Singapore, 21.6% of all primary care complaints were directly regarding long wait times, and 10.5% were about appointment booking difficulties. — [source]
  • Patients on social media highlight the absurdity of 8-12 hour wait times and people dying in the waiting room as a symptom of broken triage systems. — [source]

Problem

Patient demand vastly outstrips physical hospital capacity, causing dangerous delays in emergency care. Wait times and bureaucratic access issues account for 45.7% of patient complaints in advanced systems like Singapore, while UK A&E departments are overwhelmed by 2.3 million attendances, trapping millions in queues.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Building new physical hospital capacity takes a decade from planning to execution.
  • The demographic shift of aging populations mathematically outpaces any possible physical infrastructure growth over the next 15 years.
  • Legacy triage systems are static and do not account for real-time downstream ward availability or primary care alternatives.

AI-First Solution

A reinforcement learning system that acts as an intelligent air-traffic controller for hospitals. It ingests real-time telemetry from A&E registrations, bed management systems, and local primary care schedules to predict bottlenecks 12 hours in advance and automatically divert low-acuity patients to optimal alternatives.

  • Core: Time-series forecasting and dynamic load balancing across a regional health network's physical and digital assets.
  • Predictive Bottleneck Alerts — Forecasting A&E wait times with a measured accuracy metric to trigger early staffing interventions.
  • Digital Front Door Diversion — Integration with patient-facing apps to offer immediate telehealth or urgent care slots instead of A&E for triage categories 4 and 5.
  • Discharge Optimization Loop — Analyzing historical discharge delays to prompt care coordinators, freeing up downstream beds faster.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Average A&E wait time reduction (minutes)
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital network nodes deployed
  • Economic KPI: Cost savings per diverted low-acuity patient

  • Data Localization: Deploy on NHS-approved N3/HSCN network servers in the UK, or local secure cloud in Singapore.

3) ScribeEdge AI: Autonomous Copilot for Emerging Markets — Score: 84/100

ID: 3c4d5e6f-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f

TL;DR: An edge-native, dialect-aware AI scribe that automates clinical charting offline, combating the 2M+ global workforce shortage and clinician burnout in high-volume markets.

Evidence

  • The Americas face a projected shortage of up to 2 million health professionals by 2030, requiring joint management of workforce migration. — [source]
  • The 'Japa' syndrome in Nigeria has caused a massive crisis, with doctors leaving for the UK/US, leaving local hospitals struggling to maintain operations. — [source]
  • In Indonesia, institutional unresponsiveness led to Decree 4072/2025, forcing a 45-day limit on complaint resolution, heavily driven by poor communication. — [source]

Problem

A critical global shortage of healthcare workers forces remaining doctors in emerging markets to handle overwhelming patient volumes. This leads to rushed 5-minute consultations, severe burnout, emigration (brain drain), and profound communication failures that trigger up to 25% of patient grievances globally.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • It takes 7 to 10 years to train a physician; human capital cannot be rapidly scaled.
  • Wealthier nations continually poach talent from the Global South to plug their own demographic deficits.
  • Fee-for-service models disincentivize long, communicative visits, making empathy a structural impossibility under current volume pressures.

AI-First Solution

A lightweight, edge-deployed acoustic AI model that runs on standard tablets without requiring continuous internet access. It passively listens to patient-doctor conversations in local dialects, transcribes them, and automatically generates structured SOAP notes and patient-friendly instructions to restore eye-contact and empathy.

  • Core: On-device, low-latency Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) fine-tuned for mixed-language clinical dialogue (e.g., Spanglish, Pidgin, local dialects) working entirely offline.
  • Offline SOAP Note Generation — Creating structured clinical documentation with a measured 'time saved per visit' metric.
  • Empathy & Translation Output — Auto-generating a simplified, translated summary for the patient to take home, improving compliance and reducing complaints.
  • EHR Sync via Edge — Storing notes locally and batch-syncing them to the hospital's lightweight EMR when bandwidth is available.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Clinician hours saved per week
  • Traction KPI: Daily Active Users (Doctors)
  • Economic KPI: LTV to CAC ratio via viral product-led growth

  • Core Stack: Whisper.cpp for edge ASR, Llama-3 (quantized) for edge summarization, React Native for cross-platform app.

  • Integrations: Lightweight API connections to regional EMRs (e.g., Bahmni, OpenMRS) and WhatsApp API for patient summaries.

6. HOSPITAL DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

Security-First Architecture

Future Indian hospitals must be designed with "bunker-style" secure zones for doctors and nurses. The open-ward concept is a liability given the rise of mob violence. Front desks require physical barriers (shatterproof glass) and clear escape routes.

Automated Admin Zones

Reduce human-to-human friction in billing. Implement airline-style self-service kiosks for insurance pre-authorization to remove the Front Desk from the line of fire during financial disputes.

Intern Sanctuary Spaces

With 78% burnout and rising resident suicides, hospitals must design mandatory, high-quality rest areas for interns and residents. "Missing breaks" must be engineered out of the system.

Decentralized Agency Management

Physical infrastructure must include biometric, direct-to-hospital attendance systems for contract workers (ward boys, housekeeping) to ensure they are paid directly, bypassing agency wage theft.

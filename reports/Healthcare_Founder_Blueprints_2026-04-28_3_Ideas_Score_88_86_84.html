1) Sentinel-AI: Medical Necessity & FWA Engine — Score: 88/100

ID: e4b5d8a2-1c3f-4e9b-8a7d-5f6c2d1b9e4a

TL;DR: An AI-powered pre-authorization and claims auditing platform that stops moral hazard and ghost hospital fraud before payouts occur, unlocking billions in wasted healthcare capital.

Evidence

  • Kenya shut down 12 hospitals and suspended 40 facilities over massive fraud and ghost hospital claims linked to the Social Health Authority. — [source]
  • In Indian urban private facilities, C-sections account for 49.3% of births and 70% of hysterectomies are performed privately, driven by financial incentives rather than medical necessity. — [source]
  • US emergency admissions are routinely overbilled at rates 340% higher than Medicare rates through unnecessary diagnostic testing. — [source]

Problem

Fee-for-service reimbursement models inherently incentivize volume over value. This leads to rampant over-treatment—such as coerced hysterectomies and unnecessary C-sections—and outright fraud like ghost hospitals. This moral hazard drains billions from public health funds and pushes patients into catastrophic medical debt through inflated billing.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Auditing medical necessity requires complex, resource-intensive clinical reviews that human teams cannot scale.
  • Fraudsters adapt billing patterns faster than government regulatory bodies can implement digital oversight rules.
  • Legacy fee-for-service reimbursement structures are fiercely defended by corporate hospital conglomerates.

AI-First Solution

Sentinel-AI uses specialized LLMs and anomaly detection algorithms to parse clinical notes and claims data in real-time, verifying medical necessity against global clinical guidelines before a claim is paid. It shifts fraud detection from a retrospective 'pay and chase' model to a proactive, automated pre-authorization block.

  • Core: Multimodal LLM parsing of unstructured clinical notes cross-referenced with structured billing codes.
  • Automated necessity scoring for high-abuse procedures (e.g., C-sections, MRIs) preventing payout without clinical justification.
  • Real-time API integration with national health insurance portals (e.g., Kenya SHA, India PMJAY) and private payer HIS.
  • Provider behavior drift detection that flags clinics suddenly deviating from regional baseline intervention rates.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Percentage of fraudulent or medically unnecessary claims successfully blocked pre-payout.
  • Traction KPI: Number of payer or government health agency contracts signed.
  • Economic KPI: Gross margin on percentage-of-savings revenue model.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace (ClinicalBERT), Neo4j for provider network graphing, AWS HealthLake.

  • Integrations: FHIR/HL7 endpoints, X12 EDI 837 (Claims) APIs, integration with Epic/Cerner or local HIS equivalents.

Regulatory Path

  • Classification as Administrative/Financial Software (Non-Medical Device), avoiding strict FDA/CDSCO clinical trials.
  • Compliance with SOC2, HIPAA (US), and DPDP Act (India) for handling sensitive protected health information.
  • Implementation of strict data localization and on-premise deployment options for sovereign government health data.

  • Regulatory: Deep API integration into government health infrastructure creates massive switching costs.

2) TriageNet: Predictive Capacity & Routing Matrix — Score: 86/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a5b-8c7d-9e0f1a2b3c4d

TL;DR: An AI-driven asynchronous triage and capacity routing network that eliminates lethal wait times by matching acute patient needs with underutilized clinical nodes.

Evidence

  • The UK NHS could save 22,789 lives annually by simply matching Switzerland's 'treatable mortality' rate, highlighting the lethal nature of current wait times. — [source]
  • Rural healthcare in regions like Mexico and New Mexico is in crisis, with patients facing chronic specialist shortages and massive travel times for basic care. — [source]
  • Public sentiment and social media tracking indicates that wait times for critical treatments, including cancer, are killing record numbers of patients globally. — [source]

Problem

Public health systems are suffering from a severe mismatch between patient demand and clinical capacity. Patients with highly curable, acute conditions are dying in queues because legacy hospital scheduling systems are siloed and cannot dynamically route patients to alternative facilities or asynchronous specialists with available capacity.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • It takes over a decade to train specialist physicians, making it impossible to solve capacity via hiring alone.
  • Hospital scheduling and EMR systems are deeply siloed, preventing regional load-balancing of patient demand.
  • Misaligned incentives between public health systems and private capacity prevent fluid patient sharing.

AI-First Solution

TriageNet uses federated machine learning to predict regional demand spikes and assess patient clinical severity. It acts as an intelligent air-traffic controller for healthcare, dynamically routing high-risk patients to underutilized clinical nodes or triggering asynchronous specialist reviews to bypass physical bottlenecks.

  • Core: Predictive load-balancing and AI-driven clinical risk stratification across disparate hospital networks.
  • Treatable Mortality Risk Score: Flags patients in the queue whose conditions will become fatal if delayed.
  • Cross-hospital scheduling API integration to automatically book patients into available slots regionally.
  • Asynchronous AI triage that collects patient symptoms and vitals pre-visit to optimize specialist time.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in average wait time (in days) for critical specialist interventions.
  • Traction KPI: Number of regional hospital trusts or health authorities integrated into the routing matrix.
  • Economic KPI: Cost per patient routed vs. cost of emergency admission avoidance.

W5-W8Build the dynamic routing engine and integrate scheduling APIs for 3 partner facilities.Demonstrate programmatic ability to identify 100 open slots and match them to high-risk patients.
W9-W12Execute a live routing pilot for one specific specialty (e.g., oncology or cardiology).Reduce wait times for the pilot cohort by 20% compared to the historical baseline.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: Python, XGBoost for risk scoring, Graph databases for capacity mapping, Kafka for real-time event streaming.
  • Datasets: HL7 scheduling feeds, EMR triage notes, regional demographic and epidemiological data.
  • Integrations: Epic/Cerner scheduling APIs, NHS e-Referral Service (e-RS), local health information exchanges (HIE).

Regulatory Path

  • Classification as Class IIa Clinical Decision Support Software (CDSS) requiring MHRA/FDA clearance.
  • Rigorous algorithmic bias testing to ensure routing does not discriminate based on socioeconomic status.
  • Compliance with GDPR/HIPAA for processing and transmitting sensitive patient health data across nodes.

3) NeoGuard: Preterm & Maternal Risk Analytics — Score: 84/100

ID: f5a6b7c8-d9e0-1f2a-3b4c-5d6e7f8a9b0c

TL;DR: A multimodal AI platform that synthesizes maternal EMRs and social determinants to predict and prevent preterm births and maternal mortality.

Evidence

  • The UK could save 1,123 babies annually by simply matching Japan's neonatal mortality rate, primarily by addressing preterm birth inequities. — [source]
  • There is a systemic disregard for maternal safety in developing nations like Nigeria and Brazil, exacerbated by commodified health data and lop-sided foreign funding pacts. — [source]
  • Vulnerable demographics face severe systemic neglect, evidenced by a 41% increase in excess mortality for patients with mental health comorbidities whose physical symptoms are ignored. — [source]

Problem

Vulnerable demographics—specifically minority mothers—suffer from stark mortality inequities. Systemic biases and siloed data mean that early indicators of preeclampsia or preterm labor are often dismissed or caught too late, leading to catastrophic preterm births, preventable infant deaths, and massive NICU costs.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Systemic clinical biases often result in the dismissal of pain and symptoms reported by minority women.
  • Maternal care data is fragmented between outpatient OBGYN clinics, primary care, and hospital labor wards.
  • A lack of continuous remote monitoring leaves high-risk pregnancies unobserved between infrequent clinical visits.

AI-First Solution

NeoGuard leverages deep learning to continuously analyze longitudinal maternal health records, remote wearable vitals, and social determinants of health (SDOH). It identifies hidden risk vectors for preterm birth and maternal complications, bypassing human bias to automatically escalate high-risk mothers to specialized care pathways.

  • Core: Time-series forecasting and multimodal AI synthesis of EMR and continuous wearable data to predict maternal risk.
  • Preterm Birth Probability Index: A dynamic risk score updated daily based on new clinical and wearable inputs.
  • Automated clinical pathway escalation that directly alerts specialized care teams when thresholds are breached.
  • Bias-corrected risk baselines trained on highly diverse datasets to ensure equitable predictive accuracy across demographics.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in total NICU days for the managed maternal population.
  • Traction KPI: Number of OBGYN clinics or value-based maternity networks onboarded.
  • Economic KPI: Shared savings generated per avoided NICU admission (typically $50k+ per avoided admission).

  • Core Stack: Python, TensorFlow/PyTorch (Transformers for time-series), Snowflake for secure data warehousing.
  • Datasets: Longitudinal maternal EMRs, wearable vitals (BP, heart rate), demographic and SDOH data.
  • Integrations: Apple HealthKit / Google Fit APIs, Epic/Cerner OBGYN modules, local laboratory APIs.

Regulatory Path

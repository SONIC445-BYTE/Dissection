Evidence

Problem

High-deductible health plans and algorithmic insurance denials are creating a financial toxicity crisis in the US, pushing 100 million Americans into debt and forcing 43% to ration prescribed care. Providers lose billions to unwarranted denials, while patients are pushed to extremes, such as medical bankruptcy or legal divorce, just to survive terminal diagnoses.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

ClaimGuard AI acts as an autonomous medical coding and policy reasoning engine. It ingests a patient's EMR and the specific payer's clinical guidelines, using RAG to instantly detect unlawful or algorithmic denials, automatically generating bulletproof, medically-cited appeal letters that force payer approval.

2) FlowOS: Predictive Patient Routing to Eliminate ED Bottlenecks — Score: 88/100

ID: f5c4d0b2-8e9e-5c3b-0d2f-6e7a8b9c0d1e

TL;DR: FlowOS uses federated machine learning to predict hospital bed availability and automate patient triage, eliminating 'corridor care' in a $120B market.

Evidence

  • Long wait times outrank high costs as the global number one patient frustration. — [source]
  • UK NHS patients are waiting up to three days in A&E, creating a year-round 'corridor care' crisis. — [source]
  • Singaporean hospitals face severe bed crunches with ED wait times for ward admission stretching up to 5 days. — [source]

Problem

Aging populations and legacy hospital infrastructure have created massive access bottlenecks. In the UK and Singapore, patients face life-threatening 3-to-5 day delays in Emergency Departments waiting for ward beds. Paramedics are trapped outside hospitals for hours, unable to offload patients, collapsing the entire emergency response network.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Physical constraint: Aging populations increase demand faster than physical hospitals can be built or staffed.
  • Data siloing: Bed management is often still done via whiteboards or disconnected legacy HIS modules.
  • Clinical adoption friction: Nurses and bed managers resist opaque AI tools that don't explain their routing logic.

AI-First Solution

FlowOS is an AI-driven capacity command center. It ingests real-time HIS data, ADT (Admission, Discharge, Transfer) feeds, and local demographic data to predict bed turnover and ED surges 48 hours in advance. It automatically routes low-acuity patients to 'Virtual Wards' (hospital-at-home) and optimizes physical bed allocation to eliminate ED backlogs.

  • Core: Federated reinforcement learning on ADT feeds and EMR data to optimize hospital-wide patient flow logistics.
  • Predictive Bed Management - Reduces ED Length of Stay (LOS) by 25%.
  • Virtual Ward Triage Engine - Identifies patients safe for home-monitoring, integrating with remote telemetry.
  • Command Center Dashboard - Real-time visual heatmaps of hospital bottlenecks with actionable routing prompts.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Emergency Department Length of Stay (ED LOS) Reduction
  • Traction KPI: Hospital Trusts / Networks Deployed
  • Economic KPI: Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) per Hospital

Evidence

  • 98% of Nigerian healthcare workers face antivenom challenges, with 44% of supply delays resulting in amputations or surgeries. — [source]
  • An untrained security guard was caught performing medical procedures in rural India due to severe doctor shortages. — [source]
  • Brazil's SUS struggles massively with aging hospital infrastructure and reliance on imported medical devices. — [source]

Problem

The Global South suffers from a dual crisis: chronic supply chain failures and a massive rural-urban healthcare divide. In Nigeria, missing critical supplies like antivenom lead directly to amputations. In rural India, severe doctor shortages force untrained personnel to perform medical procedures, leading to high mortality rates. The infrastructure is broken, and connectivity is unreliable.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Massive global wage disparities drive the 'brain drain', leaving rural areas permanently understaffed.
  • Lack of reliable internet connectivity makes cloud-based SaaS solutions useless in rural clinics.

AI-First Solution

VitalEdge is an offline-first, edge-AI platform deployed on rugged tablets. It features two core modules: a predictive supply chain engine that forecasts critical stockouts (e.g., antivenom, blood) based on local epidemiological data, and an Edge-LLM clinical decision support system that guides lay-workers through emergency triage protocols without requiring internet access.

  • Core: Edge-deployed Small Language Models (SLMs) and time-series forecasting running entirely offline on mobile hardware.
  • Predictive Stockout Alerts - Reduces critical supply stockouts by 60%.
  • Offline Clinical Co-Pilot - Step-by-step emergency triage guidance for under-trained rural staff.
  • Sync-on-Connect - Automatically syncs inventory data and clinical logs to a central Ministry of Health dashboard when internet is restored.

Key Metrics

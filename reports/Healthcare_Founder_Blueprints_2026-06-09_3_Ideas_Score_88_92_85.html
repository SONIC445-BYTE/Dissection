1) TriageOS: Autonomous Waitlist Risk Management — Score: 88/100

ID: e4b2d8f1-5c3a-4a7f-9b1e-8d2c6f4a3b11

TL;DR: An AI-driven continuous triage engine that monitors public health waitlists to predict patient deterioration and auto-route them to virtual wards, unlocking $30B+ in capacity savings.

Evidence

  • 3 million people on NHS England waiting lists have received no care since their initial GP referral. — [source]
  • 49% of patients cite 'long wait times for a hospital procedure' as a primary complaint in the 10-year NHS plan insight reports. — [source]
  • Public frustration is peaking on social media, highlighting 6-month wait times and lack of physician choice in the UK. — [source]

Problem

Universal healthcare systems like the NHS are buckling under massive capacity constraints, leaving millions on waitlists without triage updates. This 'black box' waiting period leads to preventable patient deterioration, emergency readmissions, and silent deaths, while hospital administrators lack dynamic data to prioritize the most critical cases.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Legacy EMR systems (e.g., older Cerner/EMIS builds) lack real-time predictive analytics to continuously re-evaluate patient risk post-referral.
  • Severe clinical staff shortages mean it is economically impossible to manually review millions of waitlisted patient charts every month.
  • Structural rigidity and chronic underfunding prevent rapid operational pivots to alternative care models like virtual wards.

AI-First Solution

TriageOS sits on top of hospital EMRs, using NLP to continuously scan incoming GP notes, lab results, and patient-reported outcomes to dynamically re-score waitlisted patients. It identifies hidden deterioration and automatically triggers routing to virtual wards or expedited surgery slots.

  • Core: Temporal Graph Neural Networks (TGNN) for predicting clinical deterioration from sparse, asynchronous EMR data.
  • Dynamic Risk Stratification — Automatically assigns a 1-100 deterioration risk score to every waitlisted patient daily.
  • Virtual Ward Auto-Router — Integrates with NHS e-Referral Service to divert medium-risk patients to at-home monitoring.
  • Closed-Loop Outcome Tracking — Feeds post-triage intervention success back into the model to refine risk weights.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in average waitlist time for high-risk patients (days)
  • Traction KPI: Number of NHS Trusts / Hospital Networks deployed
  • Economic KPI: Cost saved per diverted emergency admission (£)

  • Integrations: HL7 v2/FHIR R4 endpoints, NHS e-RS (e-Referral Service) API, EMIS Web API.

2) ClearClaim: Predictive Out-of-Pocket & Alternative Financing Engine — Score: 92/100

ID: f5c3e9a2-6d4b-5b8a-0c2f-9e3d7a5b4c22

TL;DR: An AI-native financial clearance engine that predicts exact out-of-pocket costs and auto-matches patients to financial assistance pre-care, capturing a slice of the $140B medical debt crisis.

Evidence

  • Over 100 million Americans struggle with medical debt, with 42% delaying care to avoid further debt. — [source]
  • Nearly 1 in 5 people in the U.S. carry medical debt, with collection agencies holding over $140 billion in unpaid bills. — [source]
  • Out-of-pocket expenditure remains a massive failure point in India, acting as a primary barrier to citizen-centered care. — [source]

Problem

Patients are driven into crippling medical debt because healthcare pricing is a black box until after care is delivered. Providers suffer from massive bad debt write-offs because they cannot accurately predict patient out-of-pocket costs at the point of scheduling, nor can they efficiently match low-income patients to the billions of dollars in available but fragmented charity care and alternative financing.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Payer contracts are incredibly complex, unstructured documents that legacy rule-based billing systems cannot accurately parse in real-time.
  • Financial assistance programs (charity care, state subsidies, philanthropic funds) are highly fragmented with manual, paper-based application processes.
  • Misaligned incentives: Debt collection agencies profit from the backend failure, reducing pressure for frontend transparency.

AI-First Solution

ClearClaim is an API-first platform that intercepts the scheduling workflow. It uses LLMs to parse the patient's specific insurance policy against the provider's chargemaster to predict exact out-of-pocket costs. If the cost exceeds a threshold, an AI agent automatically identifies eligible charity care, subsidies, or zero-interest financing and pre-fills the applications.

  • Core: Multi-modal LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o/Claude 3.5) fine-tuned for extracting pricing logic from unstructured payer contracts and Explanation of Benefits (EOB) documents.
  • Pre-Care Price Predictor — Generates a legally binding 'Good Faith Estimate' with 98% accuracy before the appointment.
  • Auto-Financing Matcher — Scrapes 10,000+ local/national charity care programs and matches patient profiles via vector search.
  • Automated Prior-Auth & Subsidy Enrollment — RPA agents submit necessary financial assistance forms directly to payer/charity portals.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Total patient out-of-pocket debt prevented ($)
  • Traction KPI: Number of pre-care estimates generated monthly
  • Economic KPI: Percentage of bad debt recovered/prevented for the provider (%)

12-Week MVP

W5-W8Develop the financial assistance vector database and the patient-facing SMS/Web intake flow.Successfully match 50 synthetic patient profiles to at least one valid financial assistance program.
W9-W12Launch pilot with a mid-sized independent clinic or ASC (Ambulatory Surgery Center).Generate 500 pre-care estimates and secure $50k in upfront alternative financing for patients.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: Python, LangChain, OpenAI/Anthropic APIs, Weaviate (Vector DB), AWS Enclaves for HIPAA compliance.
  • Datasets: Historical 835/837 EDI claims data, PDF payer contracts, hospital chargemasters, charity care eligibility criteria.
  • Integrations: Clearinghouse APIs (e.g., Change Healthcare/Optum), Epic App Orchard, Plaid (for patient income verification).

Regulatory Path

  • SOC2 Type II certification for enterprise data security.
  • Compliance with Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) regulations if acting as a broker for alternative financing.

3) AuthFlow AI: Autonomous Care Pathway & Subsidy Clearinghouse — Score: 85/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a5b-8c7d-9e0f1a2b3c4d

TL;DR: An AI administrative layer that instantly resolves complex means-testing, subsidies, and prior authorizations, eliminating the bureaucratic gridlock causing care delays in wealthy nations.

Evidence

  • Patients in Saudi Arabia report severe challenges accessing timely pain management due to systemic bottlenecks despite Vision 2030 investments. — [source]
  • Singapore healthcare workers report an increase in 'difficult' patients due to frustrations with complex means-testing and bureaucracy. — [source]
  • A recent survey highlights a severe healthcare financing and reimbursement crisis in Brazil, threatening continuity of essential services. — [source]

Problem

In well-funded healthcare systems (like Singapore, KSA, and Brazil), care is delayed not by a lack of resources, but by Byzantine bureaucratic processes. Physicians spend hours navigating complex prior authorizations, means-testing for government subsidies, and fragmented Health Information Exchanges, leading to physician burnout, delayed critical care (e.g., sickle cell pain management), and highly frustrated patients.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Government subsidy tiers and insurance policies are highly fragmented and change frequently, making hard-coded rule engines obsolete quickly.
  • Data required for authorization (clinical notes, lab results, financial status) sits in siloed, non-interoperable systems.
  • Bureaucracies are inherently risk-averse, preferring slow manual review over automated approvals.

AI-First Solution

AuthFlow AI acts as a universal adapter between hospital EMRs, government subsidy portals, and private payers. When a physician orders a treatment, the AI instantly compiles the necessary clinical evidence, cross-references it with the patient's financial data for means-testing, and submits a perfectly formatted, AI-generated authorization request, reducing approval times from weeks to seconds.

  • Core: Agentic AI workflows combining RPA (Robotic Process Automation) with LLMs to navigate legacy web portals and extract clinical justification from EMRs.
  • Instant Means-Testing — Automatically calculates patient subsidy tiers by securely querying government/financial APIs.
  • Auto-Auth Generator — Drafts clinical justification letters by summarizing patient history against specific payer guidelines.
  • Denial Prevention Loop — Analyzes historical rejections to pre-flag authorization requests that are likely to be denied, suggesting clinical addendums.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in authorization/subsidy approval turnaround time (hours)
  • Traction KPI: Number of transactions processed monthly
  • Economic KPI: Administrative cost saved per authorization ($)

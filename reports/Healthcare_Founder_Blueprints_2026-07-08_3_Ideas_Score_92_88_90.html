1) ClearAuth AI — Score: 92/100

ID: e4b1b3b5-7c1c-4b9a-8f1d-2c3d4e5f6a7b

TL;DR: An AI-powered autonomous prior-authorization clearinghouse that reverses the burden of proof on insurers, unlocking a $200B administrative TAM.

Evidence

  • In Brazil, the regulatory agency ANS receives nearly 1,000 complaints per day driven by health plans denying or delaying coverage. — [source]
  • Patients face bureaucratic loops where specialist referrals expire before an appointment can even be scheduled. — [source]
  • The vast majority of healthcare lawsuits against the Brazilian public health system (SUS) are patients begging the courts for legally guaranteed medicines. — [source]

Problem

Bureaucratic gatekeeping is used by insurers and public systems to manage margins and ration care, resulting in massive delays. In Brazil, 1,000 complaints are filed daily regarding private health plan denials, while US prior authorization delays impact 94% of physicians' patients. This structural obstruction prevents patients from accessing legally guaranteed medications and timely specialist care, exacerbating illness and driving up eventual acute care costs.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Insurers have a direct financial incentive to delay care (float on capital) and deny claims to protect profit margins.
  • Clinical data is trapped in unstructured EMR notes, making it labor-intensive for hospital staff to match patient histories with constantly changing payer policy PDFs.
  • Regulatory frameworks rarely penalize insurers fast enough for delayed care, making bureaucratic obstruction a highly profitable strategy.

AI-First Solution

ClearAuth AI uses an ensemble of Large Language Models (LLMs) to autonomously read unstructured EMR data, cross-reference it against real-time payer policy databases, and generate bulletproof prior authorization requests and automatic denial appeals. By embedding directly into the hospital's workflow, it transforms a 30-minute manual nurse task into a 5-second API call.

  • Core: RAG-based LLM agents that map unstructured clinical notes to specific insurer policy requirements.
  • Auto-Auth Generator: Reduces prior authorization submission time from 30 minutes to 5 seconds.
  • EMR Bi-directional Sync: Directly writes approved auth codes back into Epic/Cerner via FHIR APIs.
  • Denial Prediction Engine: Flags missing clinical documentation required for approval before the claim is even submitted.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: First-pass authorization approval rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital departments deployed
  • Economic KPI: Cost to collect (CTC) per claim

2) Frontline Copilot — Score: 88/100

ID: f8c2d4e6-1a2b-3c4d-5e6f-7a8b9c0d1e2f

TL;DR: An offline-first, edge-AI diagnostic copilot that empowers lower-tier health workers to deliver doctor-level triage in healthcare deserts.

Evidence

  • In Nigeria, only 20% of the 34,000 primary healthcare centers are fully functional, leading patients to consult traditional medical providers. — [source]
  • Indonesia is battling 'Healthcare Deserts' where island communities are entirely cut off from basic medical infrastructure. — [source]
  • India faces a severe doctor shortage, leaving rural areas with only 1 doctor for every 811 people in heavily populated regions. — [source]

Problem

Brain drain and chronic underfunding have decimated rural primary care in emerging markets. In Nigeria, 80% of primary care centers are dysfunctional. In India and Indonesia, vast rural and island populations face severe doctor shortages. This forces reliance on traditional medicine, leading to massive spikes in preventable conditions like acute malnutrition and maternal mortality, because community health workers lack the diagnostic training to triage effectively.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Building physical infrastructure and deploying doctors to remote areas requires massive capital and decades of time, which unstable governments lack.
  • Severe global wage disparities mean trained doctors emigrate to the Global North (US, UK), leaving domestic pipelines empty.
  • Existing telehealth solutions require high-speed internet, which is unavailable in the rural 'healthcare deserts' of Africa and Southeast Asia.

AI-First Solution

Frontline Copilot deploys quantized Small Language Models (SLMs) and computer vision algorithms directly onto rugged, offline-capable tablets. Community Health Workers (CHWs) use the tablet to input symptoms and take photos of conditions (e.g., rashes, malnutrition signs). The AI provides instant, offline diagnostic triage and treatment protocols, syncing data back to a central Ministry of Health dashboard via mesh networks or occasional satellite connectivity.

  • Core: On-device, offline multimodal AI (SLM + Vision) optimized for low-compute edge devices.
  • Offline Triage Engine: Provides step-by-step clinical protocols without internet access.
  • Asynchronous Sync: Batches patient data and syncs to national health registries when connectivity is restored.
  • Supply Chain Predictor: Analyzes local disease outbreaks to predict and request essential drug restocks before stockouts occur.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Number of accurate triages completed offline
  • Traction KPI: Active Community Health Workers (CHWs) using the platform weekly
  • Economic KPI: Cost per diagnostic session vs. traditional telehealth

3) FairCost Health — Score: 90/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d

TL;DR: An AI-driven pre-care financial navigation platform that predicts true out-of-pocket costs and auto-enrolls patients in financial assistance to prevent medical bankruptcy.

Evidence

  • Patients routinely ask surgeons, 'Will I lose my house?' rather than asking about the medical risks of a procedure. — [source]
  • Medical debt is the leading cause of personal bankruptcy, accounting for 66.5% of all individual monetary defaults. — [source]
  • Patients report lowkey drowning in constantly increasing medical debt while unable to work. — [source]

Problem

In corporatized systems like the US, complex billing codes and high-deductible plans shift massive financial burdens onto patients. Medical debt accounts for 66.5% of personal bankruptcies. Patients are forced to make critical health decisions blindly, often abandoning care because they cannot get a clear price upfront, while hospitals write off billions in uncompensated care that could have been funded by charity care or structured payment plans.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Hospital chargemasters are intentionally opaque, and prices vary wildly depending on the specific insurance contract and patient deductible status.
  • Charity care applications are highly bureaucratic, requiring manual submission of tax returns and pay stubs, leading to low utilization.
  • Hospitals lack the predictive tools to accurately forecast a patient's propensity to pay before the procedure occurs.

AI-First Solution

FairCost Health integrates into the hospital's scheduling system to intercept patients before their procedure. It uses machine learning trained on historical claims to predict the exact out-of-pocket cost based on the patient's specific insurance plan. Simultaneously, an AI agent uses OCR to process the patient's financial documents (pay stubs, tax returns), instantly determining eligibility for hospital charity care or matching them with zero-interest philanthropic/fintech credit.

  • Core: Predictive pricing engine based on multi-payer historical claims data and automated financial document OCR.
  • True-Cost Predictor: Generates a highly accurate out-of-pocket estimate via SMS before the appointment.
  • Auto-Charity Care: OCR engine extracts income data from uploaded pay stubs to auto-fill financial assistance forms.
  • Fintech Matchmaker: Connects patients who don't qualify for charity care with vetted, low-interest medical payment plans.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in hospital bad debt / uncompensated care (%)
  • Traction KPI: Patient engagement rate (SMS open and form completion)
  • Economic KPI: Revenue recovered per patient engagement

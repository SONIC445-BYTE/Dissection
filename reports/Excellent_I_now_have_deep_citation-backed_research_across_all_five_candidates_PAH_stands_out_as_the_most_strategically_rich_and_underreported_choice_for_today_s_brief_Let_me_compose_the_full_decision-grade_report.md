## Subject: Daily Early-Diagnosis Brief — Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) — 2026-06-28

### 1) Snapshot (one line)

**Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)** — why early diagnosis fails in practice: A disease of the right heart that masquerades as asthma or COPD for 17+ months while standard echocardiography misses or under-reports the critical pressure signals needed to trigger the definitive gold-standard test.

---

### 2) Why Early Diagnosis Fails (3–5 bullets)

- **Biological barrier:** PAH's early vascular remodeling is hemodynamically "silent" at rest — symptoms (exertional dyspnea, fatigue) are non-specific and worsen only when ~50% of the pulmonary vascular bed is already obliterated; no single early-phase blood biomarker is PAH-specific.
- **Test limitation:** Transthoracic echocardiography (TTE), the first-line screen, fails to measure tricuspid regurgitant velocity (TRV) in **up to 41% of cases**, and omits right ventricular systolic pressure (RVSP) documentation in **62% of reports**; NT-proBNP/BNP are markers of generic heart strain, not PAH-specific vascular disease.
- **System failure — diagnostic anchoring:** COPD misdiagnosis is the single highest-risk factor for delayed PAH diagnosis (Risk Ratio ~61); patients are repeatedly treated with bronchodilators for months before cardiopulmonary workup is escalated.
- **System failure — referral gap:** Right heart catheterization (RHC), the only definitive test, requires a specialized PH center; geographic and socioeconomic barriers mean the median time from first eligible clinical event to RHC is **123 days** — and for 33% of patients, the delay exceeds **22 months**.
- **System failure — incomplete TTE reporting:** Echocardiographers frequently fail to apply the 2022 ESC/ERS guideline criteria for PH probability classification; an average of **3 separate TTEs** are performed over 2+ years before PAH is flagged, consuming 6 specialist visits and 2 hospitalizations.

### 3) Detection Window & Gap (concise)

| Stage | Signal | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Earliest detectable (research/ideal)** | Circulating microRNA signatures + exercise-induced pulmonary pressure rise on stress echo | **12–24 months** before resting hemodynamic threshold is crossed |
| **Biomarker pre-clinical window** | Elevated endothelin-1, metabolomic panel (SAM + Guanine + NT-proBNP) | **6–12 months** before symptoms become functionally limiting |
| **Typical clinical detection** | Symptomatic WHO Functional Class II–III, resting mPAP ≥20 mmHg confirmed by RHC | **17 months average** after symptom onset |
| **Gap to close** | **~15 months** of missed intervention window | Survival at 5 years: **73% (early Dx) vs. 31% (delayed Dx)** |

**Practical impact of the gap:** Each month of delay in PAH allows irreversible pulmonary vascular remodeling and right ventricular hypertrophy to progress. Closing even 6 months of this gap could shift patients from advanced WHO FC III to treatable FC II — where triple combination therapy is most effective.

### 4) What's Being Used Today (Gold Standard + Emergent)

**Gold Standards:**
- **Right Heart Catheterization (RHC):** Definitive diagnosis — mPAP ≥20 mmHg + PVR ≥2 WU + PCWP ≤15 mmHg (2022 ESC/ERS criteria). Invasive, specialist-center dependent, not scalable as a population screen.
- **Transthoracic Echocardiography (TTE):** First-line non-invasive screen — estimates RVSP via TRV. Fails in 41% of cases due to poor acoustic windows; highly operator-dependent.
- **NT-proBNP / BNP:** Prognostic monitoring standard — correlates with PVR and right atrial pressure. Not specific enough for early screening.

**Emerging Research / Tools:**
- **CorVista PH (FDA-cleared, Breakthrough Device):** AI-driven point-of-care device using 7 thorax electrodes (orthogonal voltage gradient) + finger PPG over 3.5 minutes. **82% sensitivity, 92% specificity** for mPAP ≥25 mmHg — independent of TTE imaging quality.
- **DETECT Algorithm:** Multidimensional scleroderma-specific screening tool (blood markers + lung function + ECG) with **~100% sensitivity** for PAH in systemic sclerosis — dramatically outperforms symptom-based screening in this high-risk group.
- **Circulating microRNA signatures (Nature, 2026):** Emerging epigenetic markers of pulmonary vascular remodeling being validated as an early diagnostic panel — potentially detectable 6–12 months before hemodynamic threshold.
- **3-Metabolite Serum Panel (SAM + Guanine + NT-proBNP):** High accuracy in distinguishing PAH from other congenital cardiopulmonary conditions in early-phase studies.
- **AI-EHR Algorithms (VEST — Virtual Echocardiography Screening Tool):** Flags high-risk patients from subtle ECG + EHR pattern changes **>6 months before** specialty referral.
- **Urinary NT-proBNP:** Completely non-invasive alternative to serum BNP under clinical investigation.

**Main Limitations:**
- CorVista PH: FDA-cleared but not yet widely reimbursed or deployed at scale in primary care.
- microRNA panels: Not yet clinically validated for routine use; require standardized assay protocols.
- DETECT: Validated only in scleroderma cohorts; not generalizable to idiopathic PAH populations.
- VEST: Requires EHR integration and retrospective training data — not yet a commercial product.

### 5) Where Healthcare Is Failing (Operational Insight)

**Screening point that drops the ball:**
The **primary care and general pulmonology visit for unexplained dyspnea** — where COPD/asthma is reflexively diagnosed and bronchodilators are prescribed without a cardiopulmonary pressure assessment. This is the single highest-yield failure point: a patient with exertional dyspnea and a prior smoking history gets anchored to obstructive lung disease for 12–18 months before right-heart disease is considered.

**Bottleneck most fixable in 90 days:**
**Standardized TTE reporting + automated EHR flagging.** Most hospital TTEs omit RVSP/TRV documentation (62% non-compliance). A 90-day initiative to mandate structured TTE reporting (per 2022 ESC/ERS criteria) — combined with an EHR alert that fires when a patient has ≥3 TTEs without a documented PH probability classification — could compress the "revolving door" effect significantly without new technology procurement.

**High-risk population missed:**
- **Scleroderma/systemic sclerosis patients** (15% develop PAH): Rarely enrolled in DETECT-guided screening protocols outside academic centers.
- **Young women of color with unexplained dyspnea:** Idiopathic PAH disproportionately affects women (3:1 female:male ratio); symptoms are frequently attributed to anxiety or deconditioning, delaying cardiopulmonary workup by 6–12 additional months.
- **COPD patients with disproportionate dyspnea:** The COPD label creates diagnostic closure — Group 3 PH (PH due to lung disease) is systematically under-investigated in this population.

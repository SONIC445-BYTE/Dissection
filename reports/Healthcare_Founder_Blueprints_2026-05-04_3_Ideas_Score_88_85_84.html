1) Aegis RCM: AI-Agentic Claim Pre-Adjudication — Score: 88/100

ID: e4b3c2a1-7d8e-4f9b-a1c2-3d4e5f6a7b8c

TL;DR: An AI-agentic Revenue Cycle Management platform that pre-adjudicates claims against insurer rules to eliminate algorithmic denials, unlocking billions in trapped hospital revenue globally.

Evidence

  • The National Health Insurance (JKN) is in a structural deficit with a claim ratio of 111.8%. — [source]
  • High claim denial rates by insurance companies in the UAE have escalated into a boardroom crisis. — [source]
  • US patients face a liquidity crisis with spiking insurance costs and algorithmic claim denials. — [source]

Problem

Hospitals globally are bleeding revenue due to aggressive, algorithmic claim denials by insurers, causing a 'boardroom crisis' in the UAE and massive structural deficits in Indonesia's JKN (111.8% claim ratio). This bureaucratic labyrinth traps billions in unpaid care, forcing hospitals to cut staff, delay facility upgrades, or raise out-of-pocket costs for patients to compensate for the lost liquidity.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Misaligned incentives: Insurers actively profit from complex bureaucracy and high denial rates to lower their medical loss ratios.
  • Data fragmentation: Hospital Information Systems (HIS) are siloed and lack real-time synchronization with payer adjudication rules.
  • Adoption friction: Legacy billing teams rely on manual, post-denial appeal workflows rather than preventative, pre-submission fixes.

AI-First Solution

Aegis RCM deploys a localized LLM agent that sits between the hospital EMR and the clearinghouse. It reads clinical notes, cross-references local payer contracts, and autonomously simulates the insurer's adjudication algorithm to flag and fix coding errors before the claim is ever submitted.

  • Core: Federated NLP for clinical-to-billing translation and payer rule simulation.
  • Real-time denial prediction score (Outcome: 95% first-pass clean claim rate).
  • Bi-directional API integration directly into Epic, Cerner, and regional HIS like Malaffi (UAE).
  • Automated appeal generation with medical literature and policy citation for unavoidable denials.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: First-pass clean claim rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Hospital networks deployed
  • Economic KPI: Net revenue retained per hospital ($)

2) TriageOps: Autonomous Waitlist Stratification — Score: 85/100

ID: f5c4d3b2-8e9f-5a0c-b2d3-4e5f6a7b8c9d

TL;DR: Autonomous clinical NLP platform that continuously re-stratifies hospital waitlists, predicting patient deterioration to optimize surgical scheduling and reduce wait-time mortality.

Evidence

  • UK patients face a median 30-week wait from GP referral to treatment, with thousands dying on waitlists. — [source]
  • Wait times in Saudi Arabia primary health centers are a primary driver of patient dissatisfaction. — [source]
  • Mexico's public healthcare is viewed as unsafe due to unbearable wait times, driving patients to the private sector. — [source]

Problem

Chronic underfunding and aging populations have created massive backlogs, such as the 30-week median wait times in the UK NHS. Static, FIFO (first-in-first-out) waitlists result in thousands of preventable deaths because patients deteriorate silently while waiting. Hospitals lack the manpower to manually track the evolving acuity of 7+ million patients, leading to inefficient OR utilization and catastrophic outcomes.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Structural constraint: Public systems have rigid, fixed budgets and cannot simply 'hire more doctors' to clear the backlog.
  • Data fragmentation: Waitlists are often static spreadsheets or legacy database entries disconnected from real-time primary care updates.
  • Adoption friction: Clinicians are legally and ethically hesitant to let an algorithm alter surgical priority queues without extreme transparency.

AI-First Solution

TriageOps connects directly to national/regional EMRs to continuously monitor patients currently on waitlists. Using temporal deep learning, it scans incoming primary care notes, new lab results, and wearable data to detect silent deterioration, automatically flagging high-risk patients for immediate escalation and optimizing OR schedules.

  • Core: Temporal deep learning and clinical NLP for dynamic risk stratification.
  • Dynamic Deterioration Risk Score (Outcome: 30% reduction in waitlist mortality).
  • HL7/FHIR integration with national health registries and primary care EMRs.
  • Explainable AI dashboard for surgical coordinators to approve or override AI scheduling suggestions.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: OR utilization efficiency (%)
  • Traction KPI: Number of waitlisted patients actively monitored
  • Economic KPI: Cost savings per avoided emergency admission ($)

3) AshaEdge: Autonomous Primary Care Nodes — Score: 84/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a5b-8c9d-0123456789ab

TL;DR: Edge-AI diagnostic operating system that transforms rural pharmacies into autonomous primary care nodes, capturing patients bypassing broken public clinics.

Evidence

  • 66% of Indian families bypass public primary care facilities for private hospitals, with 24% of doctor posts vacant. — [source]
  • Nigeria's OOP spending accounts for 58.3% of total health expenditure amid massive brain drain. — [source]
  • Patients in Kenya are locked out of local health facilities during nights or strikes. — [source]

Problem

In developing nations like India, Nigeria, and Kenya, there is a severe primary care deficit. With up to 24% of rural doctor posts vacant and massive brain drain, 66% of patients bypass local clinics entirely. This forces rural populations to travel long distances, incur crippling out-of-pocket expenses (58.3% in Nigeria), and dangerously overload expensive tertiary hospitals for basic ailments.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Structural constraint: Urbanization pulls medical talent away from rural areas, and building physical clinics takes decades.
  • Economics barrier: Rural populations cannot afford private doctor fees, and public systems lack funding to subsidize them.
  • Regulatory friction: Strict limits on pharmacist and nurse prescribing power prevent task-shifting.

AI-First Solution

AshaEdge deploys an offline-first, tablet-based AI diagnostic OS to rural pharmacies and community health workers. It uses edge-based computer vision for vitals and a symptom-checking LLM to guide the pharmacist through a clinical pathway, enabling them to safely triage, treat basic ailments, or escalate to an asynchronous telehealth doctor.

  • Core: Offline-first Edge AI for symptom triage and computer-vision vitals extraction.
  • AI-guided clinical pathway (Outcome: 80% resolution at the pharmacy level without a doctor).
  • Asynchronous telehealth API for instant doctor sign-off on prescriptions.
  • Epidemiological heat-mapping dashboard for local health ministries to track disease outbreaks.

Key Metrics

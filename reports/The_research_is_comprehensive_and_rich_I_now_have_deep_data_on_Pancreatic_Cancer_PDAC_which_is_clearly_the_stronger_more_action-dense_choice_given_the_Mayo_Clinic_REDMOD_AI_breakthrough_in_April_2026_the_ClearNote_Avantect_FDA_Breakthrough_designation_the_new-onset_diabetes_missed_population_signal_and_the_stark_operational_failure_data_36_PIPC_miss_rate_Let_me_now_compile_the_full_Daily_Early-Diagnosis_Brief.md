**Subject: Daily Early-Diagnosis Brief — Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC) — 2026-07-05**

## 1) Snapshot (one line)

**Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC)** — why early diagnosis fails in practice: **tumors are biologically silent until locally advanced, CA 19-9 is blind at Stage I, EUS is locked behind specialist gatekeeping, and a validated AI that detects cancer 3 years early on routine CT sits unused in most hospitals.**

---

## 2) Why Early Diagnosis Fails (3–5 bullets)

- **Biological barrier:** PDAC is anatomically hidden (retroperitoneal), produces non-specific early symptoms (weight loss, back pain, new-onset diabetes) easily attributed to GI/metabolic causes; no organ-specific pain signal until bile duct or duodenum is invaded — by which point Stage III/IV is common.

- **Test limitation (CA 19-9):** The only FDA-approved blood biomarker is elevated in only ~40% of Stage I cases; at 95% specificity, sensitivity is 68% at 1 year pre-diagnosis and drops to 53% at 2 years. Lewis antigen-negative patients (~5–10% of population) produce no CA 19-9 at all, regardless of tumor burden. It is essentially a late-stage surveillance tool masquerading as a screening test.

- **Test limitation (EUS):** Endoscopic Ultrasound — the gold standard for lesion characterization — has 85–99% sensitivity but is operator-dependent, requires a high-volume hepatobiliary endoscopist, and is concentrated at academic medical centers. Community hospitals cannot offer it reliably; referral-to-procedure time often exceeds 3–6 months.

- **System failure (Post-Imaging Pancreatic Cancer / PIPC):** In a landmark study, 36% of PDAC patients had prior CT or MRI scans where cancer evidence was present but missed. In 48% of those cases, radiologists overlooked direct tumor signs; in 28%, secondary signs (dilated ducts) were noted but never followed up operationally — losing the curative surgery window entirely.

- **System failure (new-onset diabetes triage):** Patients aged 65–85 with new-onset Type 2 Diabetes have a dramatically elevated PDAC risk in the subsequent 36 months. This signal is well-documented but has no systematic reflex screening protocol in primary care. Latinos (4×) and African Americans (3×) are disproportionately missed.

## 3) Detection Window & Gap (concise)

| Signal | Timepoint |
|---|---|
| **Earliest detectable (research/ideal)** | Radiomic CT changes (REDMOD AI): **up to 36 months pre-diagnosis** on routine abdominal CT |
| **Earliest detectable (liquid biopsy)** | Exosome + cfDNA multi-omic panel: **~12–18 months pre-diagnosis** in Stage I/II |
| **CA 19-9 elevation** | ~12 months pre-diagnosis (only ~40% of Stage I cases) |
| **Typical clinical detection** | Symptom onset → diagnosis: **median 2 months** (IQR 1–5 months) — but by then >50% are Stage IV |
| **Stage at diagnosis (current reality)** | ~15% localized, ~85% regional or metastatic |

**Gap to close:** The actionable window is **12–36 months** earlier than current detection. Closing it shifts Stage IV diagnosis (3% 5-year survival) toward Stage I (44% 5-year survival) — a **~14× survival multiplier per patient detected early.**

## 4) What's Being Used Today (Gold Standard + Emergent)

**Gold Standards:**
- **Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS) + FNA/FNB** — sensitivity 85–99%, specificity ~98.6%; gold standard for lesion characterization and tissue acquisition. Operator-dependent; academic center–only in practice.
- **Contrast-Enhanced CT (CECT) / MRI MRCP** — first-line cross-sectional imaging; misses occult early lesions (PIPC problem). Standard radiologist read has low sensitivity for pre-diagnostic changes.
- **CA 19-9 serum** — only FDA-approved biomarker; poor early-stage sensitivity (see above); used primarily for treatment monitoring, not screening.
- **ERCP** — invasive; used for biliary decompression and cytology; not a screening tool.

**Emerging Research / Tools:**
| Tool | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Mayo Clinic REDMOD AI** (*Gut*, April 2026) | Analyzes routine abdominal CT for radiomic changes; detects 73% of PDAC at median 475 days (16 months) pre-diagnosis; 3× better than radiologists on pre-diagnostic scans | Landmark validation published; not yet in clinical deployment |
| **City of Hope Exosome Liquid Biopsy** (AACR 2024) | 8 exosomal miRNAs + 5 cfDNA markers + CA 19-9; 97% accuracy for Stage I–II PDAC in multi-national cohort (US, Japan, South Korea, China) | Research/validation phase |
| **ClearNote Health Avantect** | 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (5hmC) epigenomic blood test; FDA Breakthrough Device designation | Pre-commercial; high-risk population targeted |
| **Multi-omic panels** (cfDNA methylation + proteomics + metabolomics) | Combines DNA mutation, methylation, RNA, protein markers for >90% early-stage sensitivity | Multiple academic consortia; not yet standardized |
| **New-onset Diabetes (NOD) PDAC Risk Scoring** | EHR-based algorithms flagging late-onset T2DM patients for pancreatic imaging | Algorithmic tools exist (e.g., ENDPAC score); not widely implemented |

**Main Limitations:**
- REDMOD AI: requires prospective clinical validation before deployment; needs radiology workflow integration; CT scan quality/protocol variability across sites is a confound.
- Liquid biopsies: no standardized pre-analytical protocol; exosome isolation is technically demanding; not yet reimbursed.
- Avantect: FDA Breakthrough designation ≠ approval; pending pivotal trial completion.
- All emerging tools: high-risk population definition (who gets screened?) remains unresolved at policy level.

## 5) Where Healthcare Is Failing (Operational Insight)

**Screening point that drops the ball:**
The **radiology read of routine abdominal CT scans** is the single biggest missed opportunity. Millions of abdominal CTs are performed annually for unrelated indications (kidney stones, abdominal pain, colonoscopy prep, trauma). PDAC radiomic signals are present on these scans up to 3 years before diagnosis — but standard radiologist reads are not trained, tooled, or incentivized to flag pre-diagnostic pancreatic changes. REDMOD proves the signal is there; the operational failure is that no AI-assisted second read exists in routine workflow.

**Bottleneck most fixable in 90 days:**
→ **Reflex pancreatic protocol CT + EUS referral trigger for new-onset diabetes patients aged 50+.** This requires only an EHR alert rule change and a pre-defined referral pathway — no new technology, no new budget line. The ENDPAC (Enriching New-onset Diabetes for Pancreatic Cancer) scoring algorithm already exists and can be embedded in Epic/Cerner today.

**High-risk population missed:**
- **New-onset Type 2 Diabetes, age 65–85:** Risk window is 36 months post-diabetes diagnosis. Latinos (4×) and African Americans (3×) disproportionately affected; no systematic reflex imaging protocol exists.
- **Lewis antigen-negative patients:** CA 19-9 is biologically uninformative; these patients get false reassurance from negative CA 19-9 and are not escalated.
- **Patients with incidental pancreatic cysts (IPMN):** Followed inconsistently; surveillance protocols vary widely by institution; malignant transformation is missed between surveillance intervals.

## 6) 3 High-Leverage Solution Ideas (Practical, Ranked)

### 🥇 Idea A — EHR Reflex Alert: New-Onset Diabetes → Pancreatic Risk Triage [30-day pilot]
**What:** Embed the ENDPAC score (or a simplified version) as an automated EHR alert triggered when a patient aged 50–85 receives a new T2DM diagnosis. Alert fires to the ordering PCP with a one-click referral to a pancreatic protocol CT or gastroenterology consult.

**How to run a 30–90 day pilot:**
- Partner with 1–2 primary care clinics within a health system (Epic or Cerner environment)
- Build EHR alert rule: new ICD-10 code E11.x + age 50–85 + no prior diabetes diagnosis → auto-suggest ENDPAC scoring
- Collect: (a) alert trigger rate, (b) PCP click-through rate, (c) CT/GI referral completion rate, (d) imaging yield (cysts, masses found)
- **Target metric:** ≥60% PCP engagement with alert; ≥1 early-stage PDAC detected per 200 alerts fired within 90 days
- **Cost:** Near-zero (EHR configuration only); requires a clinical champion and IT build (~40 hours)
- **Collaborators:** Health system CMO, Epic/Cerner build team, GI department

### 🥈 Idea B — REDMOD AI Integration into Routine Radiology Workflow [60–90 day pilot]
**What:** Deploy an AI second-read layer (REDMOD-equivalent or licensed Mayo Clinic tool) on all abdominal CT scans read in the radiology department, flagging cases with anomalous pancreatic radiomic patterns for radiologist review and structured follow-up.

**How to run:**
- Identify a radiology AI integration partner (e.g., Nuance PowerScribe, Aidoc, or direct Mayo Clinic licensing discussion)
- Pilot on retrospective CT archive first: re-analyze 500–1,000 historical abdominal CTs from patients who subsequently developed PDAC (look-back validation)

- **Resource checklist:** PACS integration, IRB approval for retrospective analysis, 1 radiologist champion, AI vendor contract, structured reporting template
- **Metrics:** Sensitivity of AI flag in retrospective cohort; false positive rate (radiologist override rate); time-to-follow-up for flagged cases
- **Expected impact:** If REDMOD's 73% detection rate at 16 months pre-diagnosis holds, even a 30% deployment at a large academic center could yield 5–15 additional early-stage diagnoses per year

**Why this matters:** The 97% Stage I–II sensitivity of the exosome + cfDNA panel is extraordinary — but it cannot be replicated across sites without harmonized protocols. The current literature shows 10–30% sensitivity variance from pre-analytical variability alone.

**90-day scoping actions:**

## 7) First-Principles Signal Hunt (What We Should Measure Earlier)

**Hidden signal candidate:**
- **Radiomic pancreatic texture changes on routine CT** — REDMOD shows these are present 36 months pre-diagnosis. The signal is a subtle change in parenchymal attenuation, duct caliber, and peripancreatic fat stranding that is below the threshold of human visual detection but within AI pattern recognition capability. This is not a new test — it's a **new read of an existing test.**
- **Exosomal microRNA panel (miR-196a, miR-217, miR-196b)** — shed by PDAC cells into circulation at early tumor stages; detectable in plasma with standard qPCR platforms. Not yet clinically deployed but technically accessible.
- **New-onset hyperglycemia as a paraneoplastic signal** — PDAC secretes factors (adrenomedullin, others) that impair beta-cell function before the tumor is detectable by imaging. New-onset glucose intolerance in a non-obese patient >50 is a biological signal, not just a comorbidity.

**Minimal sampling change needed:**
- For liquid biopsy: standard EDTA blood draw (10 mL) → existing phlebotomy infrastructure; no new sample type needed
- For radiomic AI: existing DICOM CT data → no new imaging required; just AI layer on PACS
- For NOD signal: EHR data already captured → no new clinical workflow; just algorithmic triage

## 10) Pattern Insight (Meta-Learning)

**Recurring diagnostic failure pattern confirmed:**
Today's PDAC brief reinforces a pattern that has now appeared consistently across multiple high-mortality diseases:

> **The "Existing Data, Wrong Algorithm" failure pattern** — the diagnostic signal is already being captured in routine clinical data (CT scans, EHR lab values, glucose readings), but the algorithm applied to that data is human-limited, single-marker, or non-existent. The failure is not a missing test — it is a missing inference layer.

This is structurally identical to:
- **Sepsis:** lactate and vital signs are already measured; the failure is that no one synthesizes the trajectory in real time
- **Atrial Fibrillation:** ECG data exists; paroxysmal AF is missed between recordings
- **Alzheimer's:** cognitive decline signals exist in EHR notes and pharmacy data years before diagnosis

**What's generalizing as a cross-disease opportunity:**
The highest-leverage intervention class is **"retrospective signal mining + prospective AI reflex"** — not building new diagnostic tests, but building inference engines that sit on top of data already flowing through hospitals. The startup/research opportunity is: *identify which diseases have the richest pre-diagnostic data footprint already captured in EHRs and PACS, and build the missing algorithm.* PDAC is the most extreme and most impactful example of this pattern currently available.

**Pattern evolution note:** Unlike Alzheimer's (where the gap is biomarker access and cost), PDAC's gap is **operational triage + algorithmic inference**. This makes PDAC more immediately fixable at the hospital level — the data infrastructure already exists. The 30-day pilot (EHR alert) and 90-day pilot (PACS AI audit) are both executable without new capital equipment or FDA clearance.

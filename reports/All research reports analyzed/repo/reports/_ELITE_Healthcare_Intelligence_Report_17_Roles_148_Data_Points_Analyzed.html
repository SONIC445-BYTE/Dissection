3. PATIENT INTELLIGENCE LAYER

Patient sentiment is highly volatile, driven by financial anxiety and systemic inefficiencies.

  • The Wait-Time Crisis: Patients wait "hours — sometimes half a day — for a 5-minute consult" [Facebook drmeghanath]. This directly correlates with violence against doctors.
  • Insurance Rejection Epidemic: 11% of health insurance claims were rejected in 2023-24 (₹15,100 crore) [Dailyhunt]. Insurers reject claims for trivial reasons (e.g., patient taking a bath at home) [Times of India].
  • Financial Exploitation: Patients face overcharging, denial of cashless treatment, and demands for bribes (₹3500 for male babies) [TOI, Reddit r/LegalAdviceIndia].
  • Strategic Takeaway: Patients are not inherently violent; they are reacting to a system that bankrupts them and makes them wait in distress. Fixing billing transparency and triage speed will drop violence rates.

6. HOSPITAL DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

 * Zoned Security Architecture: Hospitals can no longer be open campuses. They require airport-style security zoning. Triage areas must be physically separated from treatment wards to prevent 30-person mobs from reaching clinical staff.
 * Privacy-Hardened Diagnostics: Radiology and pathology wings must have biometric access to prevent unauthorized staff (like drivers) from entering and violating patient privacy.
 * AI-Integrated Waiting Areas: Physical waiting rooms must be minimized. "Using AI to simplify the outpatient service procedure can shorten the queuing time" [Frontiers in Pediatrics].
 * Staff Sanctuaries: With 91% of doctors facing burnout and interns working extreme hours, hospitals must design secure, high-quality rest areas completely inaccessible to the public.

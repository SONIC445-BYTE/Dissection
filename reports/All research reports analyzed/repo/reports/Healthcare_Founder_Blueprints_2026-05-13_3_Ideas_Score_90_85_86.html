1) AuthGuard AI: Algorithmic Denial Reversal Engine — Score: 90/100

ID: e9a7b5d2-4c3f-4811-9a2e-8f1b6c5d4a31

TL;DR: An LLM-native clearinghouse that auto-generates medically bulletproof prior authorizations and instantly appeals algorithmic denials, unlocking a $300B TAM.

Evidence

  • Up to 70% of certain insurance claims are denied to create red tape and slow down care. — [source]
  • Patients face life-threatening delays, such as denied prior authorizations for urgent lung biopsies. — [source]
  • Insurers routinely deny specific hospitals required for life-saving surgeries, even for pediatric rare cancers. — [source]

Problem

In heavily financialized systems, care is routinely denied or delayed by insurance companies using algorithmic denials to intentionally cause patient attrition. This administrative violence prioritizes profit over health outcomes, leaving 36% of US households with medical debt and forcing patients to defer critical care due to fear of bankruptcy.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Regulatory capture by the insurance lobby prevents meaningful legislative reform against algorithmic denials.
  • Data fragmentation between provider EHRs and payer policy portals makes manual appeals economically unviable for clinics.
  • Legacy Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) systems rely on human labor rather than programmatic policy matching.

AI-First Solution

AuthGuard AI ingests a clinic's EHR data and cross-references it against real-time payer medical policies using a specialized LLM. It automatically drafts highly specific, citation-backed prior authorizations and instantly generates multi-level appeal letters when algorithmic denials occur, fighting machine with machine.

  • Core: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over proprietary payer policy manuals and historical 835/837 EDI data.
  • Auto-Appeal Generator — Outcome: 40% increase in first-pass denial overturn rate.
  • EHR Note Extraction — Integration: Epic App Orchard / Cerner SMART on FHIR.
  • Payer Policy Drift Detection — Feedback loop: Alerts providers when payers silently change approval criteria.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Denial Reversal Rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Specialty Clinics Signed
  • Economic KPI: Net Revenue Recovered per Provider

2) NCD-Aegis: Predictive Chronic Care for UHC — Score: 85/100

ID: f8b6c4e3-5d4a-5922-0b3f-9e2c7d6e5b42

TL;DR: A population health AI that predicts non-communicable disease exacerbations to prevent catastrophic Universal Health Coverage bankruptcies in emerging markets.

Evidence

  • Indonesia's National Health Insurance (BPJS) is facing financial collapse dominated by NCD claims. — [source]
  • 62% of Brazilians avoid medical care due to wait times and lack of resources in the SUS system. — [source]
  • Government-funded senior care is breaking, with Medicare Advantage denying basic ambulance transfers and leaving $13,000 bills. — [source]

Problem

Nations that successfully implemented Universal Health Coverage (UHC) are now facing systemic bankruptcy. The funding models were designed for acute illnesses but are buckling under the weight of expensive, lifelong non-communicable diseases (NCDs) like diabetes and heart disease, threatening macroeconomic stability.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Shifting a national healthcare apparatus from reactive to preventative requires systemic behavioral changes.
  • Data fragmentation across regional public clinics prevents centralized risk scoring.
  • Misaligned incentives where late-stage interventions generate more revenue for privatized delivery arms.

AI-First Solution

NCD-Aegis uses federated learning across national public health databases to build predictive risk models for diabetic and cardiovascular exacerbations. It automatically stratifies patient populations and triggers low-cost, automated WhatsApp-based micro-interventions (dietary nudges, medication reminders, GLP-1 adherence tracking) before expensive hospitalizations occur.

  • Core: Federated Machine Learning for population-scale predictive risk stratification without moving raw EMR data.
  • Exacerbation Risk Score — Outcome: 20% reduction in preventable NCD hospital admissions.
  • WhatsApp Triage Bot — Integration: Direct API connection to regional public health messaging systems.
  • Adherence Feedback Loop — Feedback loop: Adjusts risk score based on patient response to automated check-ins.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Preventable NCD Admission Rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Covered Lives (Millions)
  • Economic KPI: Cost Saved per Patient per Year

3) OmniWard: AI Command Center for Rural Hospital-at-Home — Score: 86/100

ID: c7d8e9f0-1a2b-3c4d-5e6f-7a8b9c0d1e2f

TL;DR: An AI-driven telemetry and computer vision platform enabling a single nurse to monitor 50+ acute patients, saving dying rural hospitals and offsetting workforce strikes.

Evidence

  • Patient deaths rose in emergency rooms after hospitals were acquired by private equity firms due to aggressive cost-cutting. — [source]
  • Over 300 rural hospitals are facing imminent closure due to Medicaid cuts and thin operating margins. — [source]
  • Over 31,000 Kaiser nurses prepared to walk out over chronic understaffing and burnout. — [source]

Problem

A severe shortage of healthcare workers, combined with private equity financial extraction, is causing the collapse of physical hospital infrastructure. Over 300 rural US hospitals are facing imminent closure, and massive nursing strikes are paralyzing health grids, leaving millions without access to acute care.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • It takes 7-12 years to train clinical staff, guaranteeing a mathematical deficit of healthcare workers through 2035.
  • Building and maintaining new physical hospitals is too capital-intensive for local governments.
  • Current remote patient monitoring (RPM) solutions generate too many false alarms, increasing rather than decreasing nursing burnout.

AI-First Solution

OmniWard transforms rural homes into acute care beds. It uses edge AI on low-cost cameras and wearable telemetry to continuously monitor patients. Instead of raw data feeds, the AI acts as a clinical filter, aggregating vitals and visual cues (e.g., fall detection, respiratory distress) to alert a centralized nursing command center only when human intervention is strictly required.

  • Core: Multimodal Edge AI (Computer Vision + Time-Series Vitals) for high-acuity patient monitoring.
  • Alarm Fatigue Filter — Outcome: 90% reduction in non-actionable telemetry alarms.
  • Automated Nursing Scribe — Integration: Auto-documents virtual rounds directly into the EMR.
  • Acuity Degradation Alert — Feedback loop: Learns individual patient baselines to predict rapid deterioration 2 hours in advance.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Nurse-to-Patient Ratio Capacity
  • Traction KPI: Active 'Virtual Beds' Deployed
  • Economic KPI: Cost per Virtual Bed Day vs. Physical Bed

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: NVIDIA Jetson (Edge AI), PyTorch, WebRTC for low-latency video, InfluxDB for time-series.
  • Datasets: Continuous ICU vital sign datasets (e.g., MIMIC-III), annotated patient movement video data.
  • Integrations: FDA-cleared RPM hardware APIs (e.g., BioIntelliSense), Epic/Cerner Flowsheets.

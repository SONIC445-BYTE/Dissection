Dr Anant Vinjamoori, MD

Chief Longevity Officer, Superpower

Dr. Anant Vinjamoori is a board-certified longevity physician and one of the most influential clinical voices in the emerging field of longevity medicine.

Dr. Vinjamoori currently serves as Chief Longevity Officer at Superpower, where he leads clinical strategy. He is also the founder of Next Generation Medicine, an education company training clinicians in advanced longevity protocols.

Dr. Leigh Erin Connealy, MD

Clinician & Founder of The Centre for New Medicine

Dr. Leigh Erin Connealy is one of the most recognized leaders in integrative and functional medicine, with nearly four decades of clinical experience treating the root causes of chronic disease and cancer.

She began practicing medicine in 1986, founded the Center for New Medicine in Irvine, California in 1992, and later established the Cancer Center for Healing in 2009 after recognizing the limitations of conventional oncology alone.

Today, her combined 30,000-square-foot facilities are the largest integrative medical clinic in North America, having treated more than 47,000 patients from around the world. Dr. Connealy's practice combines conventional diagnostics with functional medicine, nutrition, hormone optimization, bioenergetic therapies, and advanced technologies like RGCC genetic testing, hyperthermia, and photodynamic therapy.

Dr. Robert Lufkin is a physician, medical school professor, and New York Times bestselling author whose work has reshaped the conversation around metabolic health and chronic disease.

He has served as a full professor at both the UCLA and USC Schools of Medicine, and currently holds an appointment as Adjunct Clinical Professor of Radiology at the USC Keck School of Medicine, with an academic focus on the applied science of longevity. After reversing 4 chronic diseases in himself through lifestyle medicine, Dr. Lufkin made it his mission to expose the metabolic misinformation embedded in modern healthcare.     • Actionable
    • Time-bound
    • Matched to how you actually live

    • Lifestyle adjustments
    • Supplements selected for your needs
    • Retesting timelines

## DELIVERABLE 4 — PRODUCT REVERSE ENGINEERING (complete feature/surface inventory)

### 4.A Consumer web + app surface (`com.practo.fabric`, ~16M downloads, 4.43★/273k ratings per AppBrain 2026) 🟢

Each symptom icon deep-links to `practo.com/consult/direct/new_consultation?id=N`. 🟢
2. **Find Doctors Near You** — verified-doctor discovery with specialty landing pages (`/ashburn/<specialty>`), filters, ratings, feedback. 🟢
3. **Lab Tests** — booking with "safe and trusted" framing; health packages. 🟢
4. **Surgeries** — "safe and trusted surgery centers" (secondary-care coordination wedge). 🟢
5. **Medicine ordering** — pharmacy delivery (marketplace; logistics model unconfirmed). 🟢
6. **Health articles / expert content** — SEO + trust + top-of-funnel. 🟢
7. **Practo Prime** — quality badge: ≤15-min wait, 24×7 instant booking, assured doctor (no substitute), ₹500 guarantee, free for patients (doctor fee still paid). 🟢
8. **Practo Plus** — subscription: unlimited online consults (caps: ≤5/day, ≤15/mo, 1 active/60 min), 20+ specialties, ₹399/mo or ₹2,999–5,999/yr. 🟢
9. **Practo Assured (May 2025)** — curated network of 300 hospitals / 1,200 doctors across 8 cities for quality assurance. 🟢
10. **Patient testimonials** — homepage social proof (Avinash Kumar, Amit Sharma, Jyothi Bhatia). 🟢
11. **App-download SMS capture** — homepage `+91 / Send SMS` acquisition loop. 🟢

### 4.B Doctor / Enterprise surface 🟢
- **Practo Ray (clinic PMS):** appointments, EMR, billing, SMS reminders, free basic + premium ₹1,500–5,000/mo. 🟢
- **Practo Pro (doctor app):** manage consults, calendar, patient messaging, prescriptions. 🟢 (heavy negative reviews: can't edit calendar, notification spam, video-call time restrictions, missing pediatric dosage fields — see Deliverable 16.)
- **Insta HMS (hospital HIS):** OPD/IPD, billing, claims, EMR, diagnostics, inventory, HL7/API integration; $25 (OP std) – $40 (IP std) /user/mo; 1,250+ centers, 22 countries, 18k daily active users. 🟢
- **Practo Reach:** sponsored listings / lead-gen for clinics & hospitals (monetization of free discovery). 🟢

### 4.C Retention / growth / conversion loops 🟡
- **Supply acquisition loop:** free Ray → doctor dependent on PMS → lists on marketplace → patient demand → more doctors join.
- **Demand loop:** free search → book → records stored → reminders → repeat booking → Plus subscription.
- **Referral / content loop:** health articles + app-download SMS capture.
- **AI loop (new):** 20,000+ AI calls/chats/day feeding Clinical → Consultation → Care Companion.
- **Conversion flow:** homepage symptom CTA → `new_consultation?id=N` → payment (e.g., ₹629 instant) → auto-connect to next-available doctor → prescription + records + follow-up chat. 🟢 (The auto-connect-without-choice mechanic is the center of 2025–26 "scam" complaints.)

### 4.D Security / consent flows 🟢 (from /company/security)
2FA, access zones (geo-restricted), role-based staff profiles, 256-bit encryption, HIPAA-compliant servers, ISO 27001, responsible-disclosure program (secure@practo.com).

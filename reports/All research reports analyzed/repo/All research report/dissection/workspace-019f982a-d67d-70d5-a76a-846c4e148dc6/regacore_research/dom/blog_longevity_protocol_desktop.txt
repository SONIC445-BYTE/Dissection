The Longevity Protocol: Sleep, Supplements, and Cellular Speed
June 12, 2026
7 min read
|
Written by Regacore Medical Advisory Board

How we combine blood labs and biomarker data to curate personalized, high-performance sleep and supplementation guidelines.

There is no single blueprint for longevity. To maximize physical performance and extend your healthspan, lifestyle habits and supplements must be customized to your body's unique biomarker profile.

1. Precision Supplementation

Taking random over-the-counter supplements can burden the liver and kidneys without addressing root deficiencies. Regacore maps your cellular deficiencies (e.g., Vitamin D3, B12, Magnesium, Zinc) to build a precise daily dosing plan.

2. Wearable Sleep Telemetry Integration

By pairing your wearable sleep data (REM and Deep sleep durations) with diurnal cortisol curves, we identify when sleep disruption is driven by high evening stress. Custom supplementation (such as KSM-66 Ashwagandha or L-Theanine) is timed to naturally lower evening stress hormones.

3. Mitochondrial Health and DNA Repair

Cellular energy relies on mitochondrial function. Optimizing cofactors like Coenzyme Q10 and NAD+ precursors helps protect DNA integrity and boosts cellular energy production.

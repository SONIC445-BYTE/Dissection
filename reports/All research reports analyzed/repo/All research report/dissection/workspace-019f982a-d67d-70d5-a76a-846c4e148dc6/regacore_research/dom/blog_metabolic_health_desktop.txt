Optimizing Your Metabolic Health: Glucose, Insulin, and Longevity
May 29, 2026
6 min read
|
Written by Regacore Medical Advisory Board

Why fasting insulin and HbA1c are crucial markers for metabolic flexibility, and how to improve them through lifestyle.

Metabolic flexibility—the ability of your cells to switch efficiently between burning fats and carbohydrates—is the foundation of long-term vitality. Maintaining metabolic health is essential for cellular energy and weight management.

1. Fasting Insulin: The Early Warning System

Standard checkups rely on fasting glucose, which can remain normal even as the pancreas works overtime to produce insulin. Measuring fasting insulin directly catches insulin resistance years before fasting glucose levels spike.

2. HbA1c and Glycation Damage

HbA1c measures the percentage of red blood cells coated with sugar, providing a 3-month average of glucose exposure. High glucose levels lead to glycation, a chemical reaction that stiffens blood vessels and degrades cellular membranes.

3. Steps to Restore Insulin Sensitivity

Improving metabolic health is highly achievable. Focus on incorporating regular resistance training to build glycogen storage, practicing protein pacing, and scheduling a brisk 10-minute walk after meals to blunt glucose spikes.

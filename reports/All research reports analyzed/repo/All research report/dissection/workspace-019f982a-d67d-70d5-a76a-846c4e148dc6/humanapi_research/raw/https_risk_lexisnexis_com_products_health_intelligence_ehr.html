Make faster, more informed underwriting decisions—at scale

Electronic health records (EHRs) are critical to the future of life insurance underwriting. However, many life insurance carriers struggle to adopt EHRs at scale. Consequently, they don’t achieve their desired business outcomes. Why?

Two main hurdles keep carriers from leveraging EHRs at scale. One, underwriters don’t use them as often as possible. Two, it requires work to integrate EHRs into underwriting workflows. Neither obstacle is insurmountable. However, trying to solve them in-house requires time, resources and expertise that you may not have.

What makes the LexisNexis Health Intelligence platform different?

We’ve seen first-hand what it takes to adopt EHRs at scale. We've spent years solving the problems that get in the way, so you can get moving at speed. For example:

  • We can leverage both HIPAA-authorized and consumer-mediated consent paths. Our ability to source EHRs more broadly results in higher quality, broader data than leveraging a HIPAA-authorized network alone.
  • Traditional EHRs are long, duplicative and tedious to review. Our EHR is up to 30% shorter, while retaining all the clinical information underwriters need for quick, confident risk decisions.
  • EHRs from other vendors may not include the data you want. Before we close your order, our platform scans the data to ensure it meets your specific needs.
  • Distributors can’t be as effective if they don’t have visibility into the process. Our platform enables carriers to provide transparency and streamlined communication to their distribution partners.
  • Administrative tasks distract underwriters from value-added activities. If an EHR isn’t available, an underwriter would have to manually order an attending physician statement (APS). Our solution automatically fulfills the request for an APS through our affiliate, Human API, Inc., saving time and administrative burden.
  • More than just EHRs. By combining medical data with our unparalleled identity resolution and behavioral data assets, you can unlock more sophisticated risk assessment capabilities to help you more effectively manage risk.
 

Built for life insurance carriers. Built for scale.

EHRs are critical to many carriers’ long-term business goals, but achieving those goals requires much more than the ability to access EHRs. LexisNexis Health Intelligence provides the capabilities and expertise that life insurance carriers need to adopt EHRs at scale. That means they can accelerate risk decisions, mitigate mortality risk and deliver a differentiated customer experience—and ultimately, carve out a competitive advantage.

Purpose-built outputs drive underwriter adoption 

Underwriter adoption is critical to adopting EHRs at scale.Join the elite group of carriers using our purpose-built outputs to make it easy for underwriters to make confident, informed decisions.

  • LexisNexis® Health Intelligence – Medical Insights. Receive targeted health attributes extracted directly from the EHR. Get vitals, labs, material impairments and more useful information aligned with life underwriting guidelines and risk factors.
  • Clinical History Report. Accelerate decision-making with an EHR output that’s 30% shorter while retaining all the clinical information underwriters need. Reverse-chronological ordering and date filtering make it easy to find key insights to determine risk.
  • Highlights Summary. Quickly digest an applicant’s health history, such as age, build, social history, key conditions and recent lab results. The Highlights Summary is provided at the start of the Clinical History Report, so the full EHR is available if an underwriter needs it.

Partnership that supports your changing business needs

Transformation is the culmination of many small steps, and getting started can feel like the hardest part. We’ve worked with top 10 carriers to deliver EHR data in an ingestible way that achieves their business goals and meets their technology needs. As their goals evolve, we’ve helped them expand beyond initial use cases to determine how EHRs can help them transform other parts of their underwriting function.

And because we’re at the forefront of what’s happening at the intersection of health data and life insurance, our customers can rely on us to know what’s best now—and what’s next.

Unparalleled access to health data sources boosts hit rates

We’ve spent years building integrations to health data sources, so you don’t have to. Our platform connects to over 30,000 data sources in the U.S. to provide unified health data access for over 270 million lives. This includes:

  • HIPAA-enabled EHR networks, including Health Information Exchanges (HIEs)
  • Consumer-mediated EHR networks, including Qualified Health Information Networks (QHINs) and patient portals
  • Hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and labs

LexisNexis® Risk Solutions internal analysis shows that by leveraging our networks, carriers can achieve EHR hit rates of up to 76%. Where an EHR isn’t available, our automatic pivot to APS through our affiliate, Human API, Inc. saves underwriters the time and administrative burden of ordering one manually—so they can focus on value-added tasks.

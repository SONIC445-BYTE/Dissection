Simplify the Complexities of Health Data

Personalized medicine and care require a whole person view and next-generation data collection. However, amidst this progress lies a significant challenge: the proliferation and fragmentation of healthcare data. It has never been more important to have visibility into a person’s comprehensive health history—but the data is often scattered across disparate systems. Healthcare and life science organizations need secure, user-friendly methods for patients to share their data to provide insights that can help to optimize care, advance clinical research and ultimately, improve health outcomes.

Consumer-Consented Health Data at Scale

LexisNexis® Human API™ combines a vast consumer-permission health data network with next-generation digital capabilities to address the core challenges of data access. Through this user-friendly technology, healthcare and life science companies have a transformative solution that can: 

Empower consumers with a seamless, secure and user-friendly tool to provide consent to access and share their health data, so organizations can gain a more complete picture of the individual and patient journey. 
Gain consumer-level data and longitudinal insights to deepen relationships and deliver more personalized and meaningful healthcare experiences. 
Securely access data from healthcare consumers and patients more efficiently than traditional methods.
Receive standardized and normalized data in a digital format, enabling these organizations to leverage the data for a variety of patient care, business purposes and clinical research.

Efficient Screening of Clinical Trial Patients

Clinical trial sites can conduct more efficient patient screening to improve site confidence in referrals, accelerate speed to market and support more diverse clinical research. In addition, by capturing data from diverse patient populations in real-world settings, researchers can understand treatment performance and outcomes outside of clinical trials.

Human API™ can help research teams and healthcare organizations to: 

Improve Site Confidence in Referrals:   Send the highest-quality leads to sites, reduce screen fails and site fatigue. 
Improve Speed to Market:  Enroll qualified patients more quickly with rapid access to comprehensive health history. 
Support Hybrid / Decentralized Trials:  Access consented patient data to support more diverse clinical research.


Optimize Digital Health Programs

Digital health programs can deliver personalized care plans and resources with improved patient engagement and health outcomes.

HumanAPI can help healthcare organizations to: 

Improve Patient Engagement and Satisfaction: Personalize digital experiences and deliver more meaningful healthcare experiences.
Improve Health Outcomes: Better target and deliver relevant programs to meet the needs of the patient.
Enable Data Sharing with Ease

Coupling a seamless, secure, and user-friendly consumer consent interface with a vast data network of over 30,000 data connections including medical records, labs, fitness wearables and apps, research teams and healthcare organizations can empower consumers to easily share their health data for a more complete picture of the individual and patient journey. Paired with sophisticated data management and processing, the seamless installation and flexible implementation reduces IT burden, consolidates multiple authorization modalities and data retrieval channels into a single user experience, and instantly brings standardized data into existing workflows.

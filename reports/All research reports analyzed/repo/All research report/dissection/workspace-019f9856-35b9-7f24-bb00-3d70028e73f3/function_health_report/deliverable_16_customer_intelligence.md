## 16.1 REDDIT INTELLIGENCE

### Confirmed User Reviews (Direct Quotes) 🟢

**Positive Feedback:**
- "The 1st of the 2 tests was comprehensive (160 biomarkers), and it pointed out an issue that had never appeared or even been tested before — ANA titer score was way out of range."
- "The report is also comprehensive and easy to follow."
- "They also provide comprehensive clinician notes created by their AI. These are great analyses, but you could pull any blood test report into AI and get the same thing."
- "I firmly believe this is an absolute replacement for visiting your GP annually since they do not take nearly as many lab results as function."
- "If you are a somewhat healthy individual, I do firmly believe this is and absolute replacement for visiting your GP annually."

**Complaints (Direct Quotes):** 🟢
- Scheduling: "My initial problem was with the Function's scheduler. It refused to allow me to schedule an appointment at a draw facility. Regardless of the date I selected, the system would respond with an error message stating that the chosen date was no longer available."
- Scheduling: "The automated system proved utterly useless, repeatedly redirecting me back to the Scheduling system."
- Scheduling: "The human response time was quite slow, and it took approximately three days to schedule my first blood draw."
- Results Batch: "I wish the platform displayed a list of all the tests that had been paid for, with a 'pending' status until the actual values are populated."
- Customer Service: "The Concierge decided to involve a human representative. However, the human response time was quite slow."
- Scheduling Failure: "Once again, the scheduler failed to fulfill its primary purpose."
- Incorrect Ranges: "My Magnesium levels were 4 mg/dL, which Function considers to be barely average. However, when I searched for magnesium levels in adults online, I found that 4 mg/dL is actually considered extremely high. A typical range for adults is between 1.8 and 2.6 mg/dL."
- Upsell: "Even after handing over the additional $269 they continued to hammer me for more 'add-ons'."
- Hidden Costs: "While they tout the 2x/year program, that's not really what it is. Yes, you get 2 visits for your $369/year fee. BUT, on the 2nd visit they only retest 60 of the original biomarkers."
- Retest Cost: "So, for the original 160 to be tested twice it really costs $638 — an additional 73%."
- AI Notes: "The final health practitioner notes are so AI generated it’s offensive. Nothing personal or bespoke about the write up."
- Batch Timing: "It takes them months to get the whole test to you. Months. It gets dribbled to you. They clearly want you going back to the site frequently because they are constantly trying to sell you 'add-ons'."
- Scheduling Limitations: "There is no ability to change/reschedule Quest appointments in the function app. You can only schedule the initial appointment."
- State Fees: "If you live in Ny/NJ get ready to pay tons of extra fees because of state laws. If you don't want to pay you need to get labs done in a state that doesn't have these local silly laws. We did trips to CT for our bloodwork."
- Multiple Visits: "You will need to do a minimum of 2 lab visits just to get your initial results. They market it as only two visits, which is a lie. It's a minimum of 3 to have your initial results and 6-month checkup completed."
- Data Access Blocked: "Function Health blocked customer / practitioner viewing results on Quest website — which is too bad bc Quest has lots of great options for formatting data."
- Churn: "I just quit Function Health. Suggesting that you use screen capture versus the inability to print information was very frustrating. The information provided by the Quest reports was excellent and importing them into Chat GPT and Gemini produced better analysis. In the future I am just going to order the tests direct from Quest."
- Competitor Preference: "Function health member here. My gripes are the following with Function. I know nothing about Mito though."
- Retention: "My goal here is to use function to continue to monitor my health and then go see a specialist if markers are becoming a problem."

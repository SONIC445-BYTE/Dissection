# WHOOP Complete Competitive Intelligence Bundle — v2

🟢 This bundle expands the earlier condensed report into the requested separate board-level deliverables.  
🟢 All claims are labelled 🟢 Confirmed, 🟡 Strong Inference, or 🔴 Speculation.  
🟢 Public information only; no login-gated reverse engineering, private account access, app binary inspection, packet capture, scraping behind authentication, or ToS circumvention was used.

1) AuthForce AI: Autonomous Prior Auth & Appeals Engine — Score: 87/100

ID: e4b3c79a-5f21-4b8a-9c32-1d8f5a6b7c8d

TL;DR: An AI-native engine that ingests EMR data to instantly generate bulletproof prior authorizations and appeals, reclaiming billions in provider revenue from algorithmic payer denials.

Evidence

  • 70% of commercially insured patients were initially denied coverage for at least one newly prescribed brand medicine in 2024. — [source]
  • Nearly 1 in 4 new prescription attempts remain denied after a full year of appeals due to appeal fatigue. — [source]
  • Patients experience life-threatening delays over trivial paperwork errors, such as missing signatures on forms during emergencies. — [source]

Problem

Insurers are systematically weaponizing administrative friction to contain costs, defaulting to algorithmic denials for treatments. With a 70% initial denial rate for branded medications, providers are forced to spend thousands of unbillable hours navigating complex, multi-stage appeal processes. This 'deny first, fix later' approach leads to massive revenue leakage for hospitals and actively endangers patients who abandon their prescribed treatments due to appeal fatigue.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Insurers have a massive financial incentive to deny care, as a significant percentage of patients and doctors will simply give up.
  • Lack of severe regulatory financial penalties for 'bad faith' algorithmic denials allows payers to use red tape as a profitable moat.
  • Legacy hospital billing systems are disconnected from clinical EMRs, requiring manual extraction of clinical evidence to fight denials.

AI-First Solution

AuthForce AI flips the script on payer algorithms by using Large Language Models (LLMs) to automatically cross-reference a patient's EMR clinical notes against specific payer coverage policies. It instantly flags missing documentation (like a missing signature) before submission and auto-generates highly specific, evidence-backed appeal letters when denials occur.

  • Core: RAG-based LLM architecture that maps unstructured clinical EMR notes directly to dynamic payer policy guidelines.
  • Pre-submission Auth Checker: Flags missing criteria (e.g., step therapy proof) with an outcome metric of reducing initial denial rates by 40%.
  • Auto-Appeal Generator: Integrates via FHIR with Epic/Cerner to draft customized appeal letters within 60 seconds of a denial 835 EDI file receipt.
  • Payer Algorithm Reverse-Engineering: Feedback loop that analyzes denial patterns across the network to predict and preempt future payer rule changes.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Percentage of overturned denials (Appeal Success Rate)
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital departments deployed
  • Economic KPI: Net Revenue Rescued per Provider ($)

2) ClinicOS AI: Zero-Touch Admin & Triage Agent — Score: 85/100

ID: f8d2b1e4-3c7a-49f0-b5d6-8a9e2c1f4b5d

TL;DR: An autonomous AI agent that eliminates bureaucratic friction by handling scheduling, sick notes, and virtual triage, rescuing primary care capacity.

Evidence

  • An analysis of the 937 patient complaint database in Riyadh Health Cluster 3 found complaints were dominated by administrative failures like 'Physician No Show' (117) and 'Sick Leave Delay' (112). — [source]
  • The same Saudi Arabian study found 153 complaints targeting Virtual Clinics, highlighting that poorly implemented telemedicine creates friction. — [source]
  • 55% of US adults reported that long wait times for appointments prevented or delayed their ability to seek necessary healthcare. — [source]

Problem

Primary care systems globally are collapsing under the weight of administrative inefficiency and provider shortages. Over 80% of patient complaints in major health clusters are operational—lost prescriptions, delayed sick notes, and scheduling failures—rather than clinical. This bureaucratic friction wastes clinical hours, artificially worsens wait times, and causes over half of the population to delay necessary care, ultimately driving patients into overburdened emergency rooms.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Fragmented, legacy IT systems lack interoperability, forcing reliance on manual data entry by over-stressed front-desk staff.
  • Upgrading enterprise healthcare IT is prohibitively expensive and disruptive for independent clinics and regional health clusters.
  • Reimbursement models favor procedural specialties, leaving primary care drastically underfunded and unable to hire sufficient administrative support.

AI-First Solution

ClinicOS AI acts as a digital front door and autonomous medical receptionist. Utilizing conversational voice AI and RPA (Robotic Process Automation), it interfaces directly with patients via WhatsApp or SMS to handle routine administrative tasks. It automatically books appointments, verifies identities, generates standardized sick leave certificates based on telemedicine triage logic, and updates legacy scheduling systems without human intervention.

  • Core: Multi-modal conversational AI integrated with RPA bots that can read/write to legacy, non-API healthcare scheduling systems.
  • Autonomous Sick-Leave Verification: Asynchronous chat triage that auto-generates employer sick notes for minor ailments (metric: 90% reduction in admin calls).
  • Legacy System Sync: RPA-driven integration that updates appointments in older HIS systems where modern APIs don't exist.
  • Smart Waitlist Management: AI automatically texts waitlisted patients to fill sudden cancellations, maximizing provider utilization.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Clinical Hours Rescued (Time saved from admin tasks)
  • Traction KPI: Number of daily autonomous patient interactions
  • Economic KPI: Provider Utilization Rate (%)

3) ClearCare Finance: Predictive Affordability Engine — Score: 84/100

ID: a3f9d2c1-8b4e-47a5-91f6-3c8e5d2b7a4f

TL;DR: Predictive AI that instantly matches patients with optimal payment plans, state relief, and sliding scales at the point of care, preventing medical debt and deferred treatment.

Evidence

  • Over $220 billion is currently owed in medical debt in the US, with 78% of that burden falling on individuals owing more than $10,000. — [source]
  • 42.3% of people with medical debt delayed dental care, and 23% delayed medical care, proving debt actively prevents treatment. — [source]
  • State governments are intervening; Hawaii’s Governor signed legislation to relieve $91 million in medical debt for 50,000 residents. — [source]

Problem

The systemic reliance on high-deductible health plans has created a massive under-insurance crisis. Patients cannot afford their out-of-pocket maximums, leading to $220 billion in medical debt. Because hospitals lack real-time visibility into a patient's true ability to pay or their eligibility for state relief programs (like Hawaii's $91M fund), they default to aggressive third-party collections. Consequently, 23% of patients defer necessary medical care, worsening outcomes and ultimately costing the system more in emergency interventions.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Shifting costs to patients is the path of least resistance for insurers and employers to keep premiums artificially low.
  • Hospitals lack the data infrastructure to instantly verify income and match patients to philanthropic or state-sponsored debt relief funds at the point of care.
  • Federal price caps face insurmountable lobbying opposition from hospital and pharmaceutical associations.

AI-First Solution

ClearCare Finance operates at the point of scheduling or admission. It uses machine learning to analyze a patient's soft credit data, historical payment behavior, and localized demographic data to predict their propensity to pay. Simultaneously, an AI matching engine cross-references the patient's profile with active state relief funds, hospital charity care policies, and philanthropic grants, automatically assembling a customized, affordable payment package before the patient ever receives a bill.

  • Core: Propensity-to-pay ML models combined with a real-time NLP matching engine for government and philanthropic grant eligibility.
  • Instant Relief Matching: Auto-enrolls eligible patients into state debt relief or hospital charity care (metric: % of bad debt converted to funded care).
  • Dynamic Payment Plans: Generates personalized, zero-interest installment plans based on predicted cash flow at the point of care.
  • Fintech EMR Integration: Embeds directly into Epic MyChart so patients see their 'true, funded cost' before confirming an appointment.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in Hospital Bad Debt / Uncompensated Care (%)
  • Traction KPI: Patient Opt-In Rate for Financing/Grants
  • Economic KPI: Gross Transaction Volume (GTV) processed

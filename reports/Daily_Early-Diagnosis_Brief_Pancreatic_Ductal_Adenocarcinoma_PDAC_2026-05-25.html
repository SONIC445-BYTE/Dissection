Subject: Daily Early-Diagnosis Brief — Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC) — 2026-05-25

1) Snapshot (one line)

Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma (PDAC) — why early diagnosis fails in practice: The disease hides anatomically and its earliest systemic signal is routinely mismanaged as standard Type 2 Diabetes, missing the critical window before metastasis.

2) Why early diagnosis fails (3–5 bullets)

• Biological barrier: Deep anatomical location prevents palpable masses; early tumors are asymptomatic until they invade local nerves or obstruct the bile duct.

• Test limitation: The standard biomarker (CA19-9) is highly non-specific (elevated in benign biliary conditions), and standard-of-care non-contrast CTs routinely miss pre-diagnostic lesions.

• System failure: Primary care systems treat New-Onset Diabetes (NOD) in adults over 50 as a routine metabolic issue, failing to recognize it as a paraneoplastic symptom of early PDAC.

3) Detection window & gap (concise)

Earliest detectable signal (research / ideal): 18–36 months prior to clinical symptoms via cfDNA/exosome biomarkers or retrospective AI analysis of opportunistic CTs.

Typical clinical detection: Stage III/IV (locally advanced or metastatic) driven by jaundice, severe back pain, or weight loss.

Gap to close: 1.5 to 3 years. Closing this gap shifts resectability rates from <20% to >70%, fundamentally altering the survival curve.

4) What’s being used today (gold standard + emergent)

Gold standard(s): High-resolution multi-phase pancreatic protocol CT, Endoscopic Ultrasound (EUS), CA19-9 blood test.

Emerging research / tools: Avantect® Pancreatic Cancer Test (ClearNote Health) for high-risk NOD cohorts; PANORAMA and PANDA AI models for opportunistic non-contrast CT screening; cfDNA and exosome-based multi-marker panels (Nature Comms 2025).

Main limitations: Mass population screening is economically unviable due to low prevalence; EUS is invasive and operator-dependent; imaging requires high clinical suspicion to even be ordered.

5) Where healthcare is failing (operational insight)

Screening point that drops the ball: The primary care / endocrinology handoff. Adults >50 presenting with glycemically defined new-onset diabetes have a 5-fold higher risk of PDAC within 3 years, yet they almost never receive reflex cancer screening.

Bottleneck most fixable in 90 days: Automating risk stratification in the EHR for new diabetes diagnoses to trigger targeted screening.

High-risk population missed: Patients >50 with rapid-onset diabetes, slight unexplained weight loss, or familial genetic risk (BRCA, ATM) who are currently lost in routine primary care workflows.

6) 3 High-leverage solution ideas (practical, ranked)

[Idea A — quick pilotable] — EHR Reflex for the END-PAC Score. How to run a 90-day pilot: Build an automated logic rule in Epic/Cerner that calculates the END-PAC (Enriching New-onset Diabetes for Pancreatic Cancer) score for all new adult diabetes diagnoses. If score ≥3, trigger an automated EUS/CT or liquid biopsy referral. Metrics to collect: % of NOD patients successfully screened, pre-malignant lesions (IPMNs) found, and clinical adoption rate.

[Idea B — scalable tech / workflow change] — Opportunistic AI on Incidental CTs. Resource checklist: API integration with a validated radiologic AI vendor (e.g., PANDA model tech), PACS integration, and a designated follow-up coordinator. Expected impact: Running the AI in the background on all non-contrast abdominal/chest CTs ordered in the ER for unrelated issues will catch asymptomatic early PDACs missed by the human eye at zero additional scanning cost.

[Idea C — research / product] — Localized NOD-to-Liquid Biopsy Pipeline. Highest upside: Partner with a commercial assay like ClearNote Health to bypass imaging bottlenecks entirely. Tests needed: Validate cfDNA/exosome testing directly at the point of HbA1c diagnosis. Collaborators to approach: Regional endocrinology clinics and primary care networks.

7) First-principles signal hunt (what we should measure earlier)

Hidden signal candidate: Paraneoplastic metabolic shift (tumor-derived exosomes inducing peripheral insulin resistance) that precedes actual tumor bulk.

Minimal sampling change needed: Diverting 10mL of serum from the routine blood draw already being taken for the initial HbA1c/metabolic panel.

10) Pattern Insight (Meta-Learning)

What recurring diagnostic failure pattern is emerging? Deadly, fast-moving diseases are routinely misclassified at their onset as common, chronic conditions by primary care.

Is today’s disease reinforcing or breaking that pattern? Strongly reinforcing. PDAC masquerades as standard Type 2 Diabetes, exploiting the healthcare system's habit of treating chronic metabolic symptoms without questioning their underlying etiology.

What generalizable opportunity is forming across diseases? Building "EHR Interceptors"—software that monitors routine primary care data for sudden-onset chronic conditions (e.g., diabetes, hypertension, asthma) in atypical demographics, automatically converting them into triggers for rare or severe disease screening.

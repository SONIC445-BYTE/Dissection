6. Hospital Design Implications for the Future

  • Zonal Security Architecture: Security cannot be limited to external gates. Casualty and ICU wards must have physical barrier designs, secure triage windows, and rapid-exit routes for staff to escape mob violence.
  • Barrier-Free Accessibility: Compliance with universal accessibility standards for physical infrastructure (as highlighted in Delhi hospital studies).
  • De-escalation Waiting Rooms: Redesigning ED waiting areas with transparent digital boards showing queue status and AI-estimated wait times to reduce psychological friction.
  • Integrated Rest Zones: Mandatory, secure sleep and rest areas for interns and residents who are working 28-hour shifts without sleep.

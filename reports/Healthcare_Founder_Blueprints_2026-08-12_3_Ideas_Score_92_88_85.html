1) Aegis Health AI — Score: 92/100

ID: e8b49e1a-5b7a-4c2d-8a9d-1234567890ab

TL;DR: An AI-native patient advocacy platform that weaponizes LLMs to counter algorithmic insurance denials and eliminate medical debt, attacking a $50B+ TAM.

Evidence

  • A family's insurance denied residential mental health treatment for their son following a suicide attempt, with the insurer claiming the level of care was 'not medically necessary.' — [source]
  • Routine but necessary procedures like root canals are frequently denied or capped, forcing patients into debt. — [source]
  • Federal protections against medical debt reporting rolled back in 2025/2026, leaving patients relying on a confusing patchwork of state laws. — [source]

Problem

Medical debt is the single largest source of debt collection in the US, affecting 100 million Americans. Insurers aggressively use algorithmic denials (often citing 'not medically necessary') to optimize their revenue cycles. Even fully insured patients face financial ruin, with 68% of medical debt holders stating they expected their insurance to cover the bills that drove them into default.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • The US healthcare lobbying apparatus is deeply entrenched, preventing structural payer reform.
  • The massive Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) industry relies on and profits from this billing complexity.
  • A transition to a single-payer system lacks political viability, leaving patients to navigate state-by-state legal patchworks.

AI-First Solution

A patient-side AI advocate that ingests EMR data, billing codes, and denial letters, cross-references them against payer guidelines and state laws, and auto-generates HIPAA-compliant, clinically rigorous appeal letters to overturn algorithmic denials.

  • Core: RAG-based LLM fine-tuned on medical necessity criteria and state-level healthcare debt legislation.
  • Automated Appeal Generation — Outcome metric: % of denials overturned.
  • Patient Portal Integration — Pulls directly from Epic MyChart and 835/837 EDI files.
  • Payer Denial Pattern Recognition — Feedback loop that predicts which codes a specific payer will deny and preemptively structures the clinical justification.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Total dollar amount of medical debt reversed/prevented
  • Traction KPI: Number of self-insured employer contracts signed
  • Economic KPI: LTV to CAC ratio (targeting 4:1+)

2) TriageNode AI — Score: 88/100

ID: f9c5a2b3-6d8e-4f1a-9c0d-2e3f4a5b6c7d

TL;DR: Predictive patient flow AI that dynamically re-routes waitlists to prevent clinical deterioration in universal healthcare systems, addressing a $20B capacity collapse.

Evidence

  • As of March 2026, the NHS continues to miss the target of 80% of patients receiving a cancer diagnosis outcome within 28 days of an urgent referral, hovering around 79.4%. — [source]
  • The number of patients waiting over 12 hours for an emergency admission in June 2026 was about 107 times higher than pre-pandemic levels in 2019. — [source]
  • 22% of patients were waiting more than six weeks for diagnostic tests in mid-2025, far above the 1% standard. — [source]

Problem

Universal healthcare systems like the NHS are experiencing a capacity collapse. With 7.28 million cases on the waitlist and 12-hour A&E delays, static triage systems fail. Patients deteriorate while trapped on waitlists, turning elective issues into emergency admissions, which further clogs the system and leads to preventable mortality.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Fixing capacity requires massive, sustained capital injection into health and social care.
  • Aging demographics outpace the slow, decade-long pipeline required to train new clinicians.
  • Legacy IT systems lack cross-trust interoperability, preventing dynamic load balancing of patients.

AI-First Solution

A federated machine learning platform that monitors waitlisted patients via asynchronous digital check-ins and EMR data. It predicts clinical deterioration risk in real-time, automatically up-triageing high-risk patients and re-routing them to available diagnostic slots across the regional network.

  • Core: Federated time-series predictive modeling on patient waitlist (PTL) data to forecast deterioration.
  • Dynamic Risk Stratification — Outcome metric: % reduction in waitlist-to-emergency conversions.
  • NHS Spine & e-Referral Integration — Pulls live capacity data across regional Integrated Care Boards (ICBs).
  • Automated Asynchronous Patient Check-ins — NLP-driven SMS feedback loop to capture worsening symptoms.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in waitlist-driven emergency admissions
  • Traction KPI: Number of NHS Trusts / ICBs deployed
  • Economic KPI: Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) per Trust

ID: a1b2c3d4-5e6f-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d

TL;DR: An edge-AI clinical co-pilot that empowers rural nurses in developing nations to perform specialist-level diagnostics, solving a $35B staffing gap.

Evidence

  • Indian public hospitals suffer from 'doctor shortages, medicine gaps, endless waiting lines,' forcing the poorest citizens to pay out-of-pocket for private care. — [source]
  • Despite a surplus of medical seats, doctors complain of poor pay and infrastructure in rural public postings, refusing them and clustering in urban private centers. — [source]
  • Poor emergency care and doctor shortages are crippling major government hospitals in Hyderabad, leaving trauma patients without immediate care. — [source]

Problem

Rapidly developing nations like India are building massive hospital infrastructure but face a 1:11,000 doctor-to-patient gap in public facilities. Doctors refuse rural postings due to poor pay, leaving massive government hospitals practically useless. Consequently, trauma and emergency care in rural areas is crippled, forcing patients into expensive private care or resulting in preventable deaths.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Brain drain to Western nations and lucrative urban private sectors depletes the public talent pool.
  • It takes a decade to train a medical specialist, making it impossible to scale human capital as fast as concrete infrastructure.
  • Governments cannot drastically increase public sector salaries without triggering macroeconomic debt crises.

AI-First Solution

An offline-first, edge-deployable multi-modal AI (vision + NLP) running on standard tablets. It acts as a clinical co-pilot for rural nurses and junior clinical officers, guiding them through trauma triage, basic ultrasound interpretation, and differential diagnosis without requiring cloud connectivity or specialist presence.

  • Core: Edge-native multi-modal LLM and Computer Vision models optimized for low-compute Android devices.
  • Offline Trauma & Diagnostic Triage — Outcome metric: % of cases resolved locally without tertiary transfer.
  • ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) Integration — Syncs patient records to national registries when connectivity is restored.
  • Asynchronous Specialist Review — Flags low-confidence edge predictions for urban specialist review, creating a continuous feedback loop.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Number of successful rural case resolutions (avoided unnecessary tertiary transfers)
  • Traction KPI: Number of rural Primary Health Centres (PHCs) deployed
  • Economic KPI: Hardware + SaaS gross margin

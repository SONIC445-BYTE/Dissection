## Subject: Daily Early-Diagnosis Brief — Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) — 2026-07-03

### 1) Snapshot (one line)

**Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)** — why early diagnosis fails in practice: a fatal 2–4 year diagnostic lag driven by symptom mimicry, echocardiography blind spots, and a broken primary-care-to-specialist referral chain that allows irreversible vascular remodeling to occur silently before any alarm is raised.

---

### 2) Why Early Diagnosis Fails (3–5 bullets)

- **Biological barrier:** PAH's early haemodynamic changes (rising pulmonary vascular resistance, right ventricular remodeling) are clinically silent — dyspnea and fatigue are indistinguishable from asthma, deconditioning, or anxiety; the disease is biologically "hidden" until the right ventricle begins to fail overtly
- **Test limitation:** The primary screening tool — transthoracic echocardiography (TTE) — cannot measure tricuspid regurgitant velocity (TRV) in **up to 41% of cases**, meaning TTE reports routinely omit right ventricular systolic pressure (RVSP) entirely, killing the referral trigger; right heart catheterization (RHC, the gold standard) is invasive and skipped in ~25% of suspected cases even against guidelines
- **System failure — misdiagnosis cascade:** Mayo Clinic data (DuBrock et al., Dec 2025, PMC12705487) shows COPD misdiagnosis carries a **Risk Ratio of 61.1** for delayed PAH diagnosis; acute respiratory infection misdiagnosis RR = 7.8; normal creatinine (false reassurance) RR = 32.2 — each "normal" result paradoxically delays workup
- **System failure — referral bottleneck:** Patients average **3 TTEs, 6 specialist visits, and 2 hospitalizations** before reaching diagnosis (JACC Case Reports, Sept 2025); referral to tertiary PH centers is inconsistent and geographically unequal
- **Screening policy gap:** Scleroderma/systemic sclerosis patients carry a **≤25% lifetime PAH risk** and guidelines mandate annual echocardiography — yet compliance is inconsistently enforced; this high-yield cohort is being systematically under-screened in community rheumatology settings

### 3) Detection Window & Gap (concise)

| Stage | Timepoint / Marker |
|---|---|
| **Earliest detectable signal (research/ideal)** | Elevated pulmonary vascular resistance on exercise RHC + plasma GDF-15 elevation + AI-ECG signal — potentially **12–18 months before clinical presentation** |
| **Typical clinical detection** | Median **22.2 months** from symptom onset (Mayo Clinic registry, DuBrock 2025); some patients exceed **3–4 years** |
| **Gap to close** | **~18–36 months** — during which irreversible pulmonary vascular remodeling progresses; the cost is a **42-percentage-point difference in 5-year survival** (73% early vs. 31% delayed) |

**Practical impact of the gap:** Every month of delay is not linear — vascular remodeling accelerates non-linearly; patients who cross the threshold into RV failure have a fundamentally altered disease trajectory that no current therapy fully reverses.

### 4) What's Being Used Today (Gold Standard + Emergent)

**Gold Standards:**
- **Right Heart Catheterization (RHC):** Definitive — measures mean pulmonary artery pressure (mPAP ≥20 mmHg), pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR ≥2 WU), pulmonary artery wedge pressure (≤15 mmHg). Invasive; not suitable for screening; skipped in ~25% of cases
- **Transthoracic Echocardiography (TTE):** Primary non-invasive screen — estimates RVSP via TRV, assesses RV size/function. Fails in 41% of cases due to poor acoustic windows; high operator dependency; commonly misread in non-specialist settings
- **BNP / NT-proBNP:** Functional severity marker; used for risk stratification, not early detection; elevated only after significant haemodynamic compromise

**Emerging Research / Tools (2025–2026):**
| Tool | Type | Key Performance |
|---|---|---|
| **Anumana ECG-AI** (FDA 510(k) cleared, March 28, 2026) | AI on standard 12-lead ECG | Sensitivity 73.0%, Specificity 74.4% — detects hidden RV strain signals |
| **CorVista PH System** (FDA Breakthrough Device) | Non-invasive POC (thorax electrodes + PPG) | Sensitivity **82%**, Specificity **92%** — no technician required |
| **GDF-15 / Plasma Proteomics (CIPHER studies)** | Blood biomarker | Early RV remodeling signal; validation ongoing in multicenter cohort |
| **CT Chest Radiomics** | Imaging AI | Quantitative pulmonary vascular features from routine CT; predicts diagnosis + prognosis |
| **CIPHER-MRI** | Cardiac MRI + biomarker pairing | Detects early RV remodeling phenotypes; research phase |
| **Point-of-care NT-proBNP** | POC blood test | 92% correct classification in primary care triage (JTD 2025) |
| **Satralizumab (immune PAH subtype)** | Therapy-linked biomarker | Immune activation markers identify a treatable PAH subtype (AHA 2025 trial data) |

**Main Limitations:** Anumana ECG-AI's 73–74% sensitivity/specificity is insufficient as a standalone screen (too many false negatives/positives at population scale); CorVista is promising but requires prospective validation in primary care; GDF-15 and proteomic panels are not yet standardized or commercially available; CT radiomics requires expert post-processing pipelines.

### 5) Where Healthcare Is Failing (Operational Insight)

**Screening point that drops the ball:**
The primary care encounter is the critical failure node. GPs and internists receive a TTE report with no RVSP measurement (TRV unmeasurable in 41% of studies) and interpret this as "echocardiogram normal" — terminating the referral pathway. The patient is then treated for asthma or deconditioning, consuming 1–2 years before re-presentation.

**Bottleneck most fixable in 90 days:**
> **Structured TTE reporting mandate + reflex NT-proBNP protocol.** If a TTE report cannot measure TRV, the reporting cardiologist/sonographer should be required to flag "TRV unmeasurable — PH cannot be excluded — consider NT-proBNP + PH center referral." This single workflow change costs nothing and could eliminate the single largest referral-killing event. Implementable via EHR structured reporting template modification in any hospital system within 30–60 days.

**High-risk population missed:**
- **Young women (20–45):** Symptoms attributed to anxiety, poor fitness, or functional dyspnea; PAH disproportionately affects this demographic (female-to-male ratio ~3:1 in idiopathic PAH); misdiagnosis delay is longest in this group
- **Scleroderma / CTD patients in community rheumatology:** Annual echo screening mandated by guidelines but non-compliance is endemic outside academic centers — a high-yield, identifiable cohort being systematically missed
- **Physician experience paradox (ERJ Open Research, Dardi et al., Feb 2026):** Less experienced physicians (2–10 years) outperformed senior clinicians in diagnostic accuracy — indicating senior physicians are not updating practice to 2022 ESC/ERS guidelines; targeted CME is an immediate lever

### 10) Pattern Insight (Meta-Learning)

**Recurring diagnostic failure pattern emerging:**

> 📌 **"The Normal-Test Trap"** — A disease with early, non-specific symptoms gets evaluated by a standard test that is structurally incapable of detecting it at the early stage — and a *negative* or *inconclusive* result is misread as *reassuring* rather than *uninformative*. This terminates the diagnostic workup.

This is the **same pattern** seen in:
- **Pancreatic cancer** → CT scan is "normal" in early-stage PDAC → patient reassured → detected at Stage III/IV
- **MASLD/MASH** → LFTs normal until advanced fibrosis → no further workup
- **ALS** → EMG "borderline" early → neurologist reassured → re-presents 10 months later

PAH is today's clearest instantiation: TTE cannot measure TRV in 41% of cases → report says nothing → GP reassured → patient treated for asthma for 2 years.

**Generalizable opportunity forming:**
> **"Uninformative ≠ Negative" — The next generation of diagnostic value lies not in building better tests from scratch, but in adding structured interpretive logic to existing tests that currently produce false reassurance.** The EHR-level intervention (flagging test limitations, triggering reflex orders) is a near-zero-cost, hospital-implementable lever that precedes and enables any AI/biomarker innovation. This is a repeatable playbook: identify the standard test that produces false reassurance → add a structured flag + reflex order → measure referral rate change. Applicable across PAH, PDAC, MASLD, early ALS, and ovarian cancer screening.

**Pattern verdict:** Today's disease **strongly reinforces** the Normal-Test Trap pattern. The generalizable startup/hospital opportunity is a **"Diagnostic Uncertainty Flagging Layer"** — software that sits on top of EHR lab/imaging reports and automatically classifies results as *Positive / Negative / **Uninformative*** — routing "Uninformative" results to reflex testing protocols rather than silent dismissal.

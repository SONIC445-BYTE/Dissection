1) AeroTriage: Predictive Patient Flow OS — Score: 88/100

ID: d3b07384-d9a7-47b1-91a5-8e3b2c12a456

TL;DR: An AI-native predictive logistics engine that eliminates A&E bottlenecks by automating bed allocation, solving the $500B public infrastructure collapse.

Evidence

  • Over 13,000 patients in England experienced A&E wait times exceeding three days, creating a deadly corridors crisis. — [source]
  • Citizens globally report that systems are being flooded, leaving them unable to get basic care or see a doctor. — [source]
  • Bureaucratic rules and failing infrastructure in public systems directly result in preventable patient deaths, such as the asphyxiation of Leonardo Barcellos. — [source]

Problem

Public healthcare systems in the UK and Brazil are buckling under chronic underfunding and exponential demand, resulting in fatal wait times and overcrowded emergency departments. With over 13,000 patients in the UK waiting more than three days in A&E, patients are dying in hospital corridors. The manual, reactive nature of bed management and triage exacerbates physical infrastructure limits, creating a deadly bottleneck that hurts patients, burns out staff, and incurs massive liability.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Building physical hospital infrastructure and training new specialists takes a decade minimum.
  • Political gridlock prevents the massive tax increases required to properly fund and expand these public monopolies.
  • Legacy EMR systems (Epic, Cerner) act as static databases rather than real-time, predictive logistics engines.

AI-First Solution

AeroTriage acts as an air-traffic control system for hospitals. It ingests real-time HL7 ADT (Admission, Discharge, Transfer) feeds and EMR clinical notes to predict bed availability 48 hours in advance and dynamically route A&E patients to the optimal ward, bypassing manual bottleneck triage.

  • Core: Time-series forecasting and NLP for predictive discharge and automated clinical risk scoring.
  • Predictive Bed Management: Reduces A&E wait times by forecasting discharges with 90%+ accuracy.
  • HL7/FHIR Integration: Plugs directly into existing Trust integration engines without ripping out the underlying EMR.
  • Continuous Feedback Loop: Model retrains weekly on actual discharge times vs. predicted times to adjust for seasonal/staffing variations.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in A&E Left Without Being Seen (LWBS) rate.
  • Traction KPI: Number of NHS Trusts or hospital networks signed for pilots.
  • Economic KPI: Annual Contract Value (ACV) and gross margin per deployment.

2) NerveNet AI: Clinical Copilot for the Global South — Score: 85/100

ID: f4c18495-e0b8-48c2-a2b6-9f4c3d23b567

TL;DR: An offline-first clinical decision support API that empowers nurses to diagnose at a physician level, countering the $250B brain drain crisis.

Evidence

  • Over 16,000 doctors have left Nigeria in the last five years, causing systemic collapse and strikes over unpaid wages. — [source]
  • Continuous friction in Kenya over the government's failure to post medical interns and upgrade infrastructure leads to constant strikes. — [source]
  • Social media sentiment highlights an accountability crisis in Nigeria where infrastructure hasn't been meaningfully upgraded for years. — [source]

Problem

A massive medical workforce exodus is paralyzing healthcare in the Global South. With over 16,000 doctors leaving Nigeria recently and the WHO projecting a 10 million global health worker shortfall by 2030, local clinics are left entirely unstaffed. Task-shifting to nurses and community health workers (CHWs) is the only mathematical solution, but these workers lack the clinical training to safely diagnose and prescribe without physician oversight.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Massive global wage disparities pull doctors to Western nations; local governments cannot match salaries.
  • Medical education takes 10+ years, meaning the pipeline cannot replenish fast enough.
  • Existing clinical decision support tools require high-speed internet and target Western epidemiology, failing in low-resource, offline environments.

AI-First Solution

NerveNet AI is an edge-deployed Small Language Model (SLM) trained specifically on local epidemiology, WHO guidelines, and essential medicine lists. It acts as an interactive diagnostic copilot on low-end Android devices, guiding nurses through structured clinical reasoning without requiring an internet connection.

  • Core: Offline-first, quantized LLM inference optimized for mobile devices.
  • Diagnostic Pathway Generation: Recommends evidence-based triage and treatment plans based on symptom input.
  • Edge EMR Sync: Stores encounter data locally and syncs to a central cloud EMR via SMS/USSD or when WiFi is available.
  • Continuous Localization: Model updates are pushed over-the-air, incorporating local outbreak data (e.g., localized malaria resistance).

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Percentage of patient cases safely resolved by a nurse/CHW without physician escalation.
  • Traction KPI: Number of active community clinics deployed.
  • Economic KPI: Cost per diagnosis / API call equivalent.

3) EquiClaim: UHC Empanelment & Factoring Engine — Score: 91/100

ID: a5d295a6-f1c9-49d3-b3c7-0e5d4e34c678

TL;DR: An AI clearinghouse and micro-factoring platform that makes government insurance profitable for private hospitals, unlocking $150B in dormant UHC capital.

Evidence

  • Only 13% of private hospitals in Maharashtra are empanelled in government insurance schemes due to drastically lower reimbursement rates ($299 vs $955 for hernia surgery). — [source]
  • Rural districts with high tribal populations have almost zero empanelled private hospitals, forcing the poor to pay out-of-pocket despite holding insurance cards. — [source]
  • Systemic failures in public care laws lead to political promises of coverage that do not translate into actual hospital beds or access. — [source]

Problem

Governments in emerging markets (India, Mexico) launch massive Universal Health Coverage (UHC) schemes, but private hospitals boycott them. In India, 87% of private hospitals refuse PMJAY patients because government reimbursement rates are vastly below cost, and corrupt, manual claims processing delays payments for months. This leaves millions with 'coverage' on paper but no access to care, driving catastrophic out-of-pocket spending.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Governments lack the tax base to pay private market rates, keeping reimbursements artificially low.
  • Private hospitals operate on razor-thin margins and cannot float the working capital required to wait 6+ months for government payouts.
  • Manual claims processing by government TPAs (Third Party Administrators) is prone to arbitrary rejections and corruption.

AI-First Solution

EquiClaim provides an AI-driven claims scrubber and instant factoring engine. It uses NLP to auto-code clinical notes to ensure 100% compliance with government UHC rules, instantly predicts the probability of claim approval, and offers hospitals immediate cash payouts (factoring) for approved claims, eliminating working capital constraints.

  • Core: NLP-driven medical coding and predictive risk modeling for instant financial factoring.
  • Auto-Coder: Translates raw clinical notes into compliant UHC billing codes, eliminating manual errors and rejections.
  • Predictive Factoring API: Assesses claim validity in real-time and offers 80% instant cash advance to the hospital.
  • Cost-Optimization Dashboard: Recommends clinical pathway adjustments to help hospitals achieve profitability even at low government reimbursement rates.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Volume of UHC claims successfully processed and funded.
  • Traction KPI: Number of new private hospitals empanelled into UHC via the platform.
  • Economic KPI: Factoring spread (Net Interest Margin) + SaaS fees per claim.

1) ClearAuth AI: Autonomous Prior Authorization — Score: 91/100

ID: e4b1a8d2-5f3c-4a11-b9c7-9e8d7a6b5c4d

TL;DR: An autonomous multi-modal AI agent that eliminates $265B in US administrative waste by instantly translating unstructured EMR notes into approved prior authorizations.

Evidence

  • Patients face 25% YoY premium increases and aggressive prior authorization denials that delay life-saving care. — [source]
  • A patient received a surprise $37,000 ER bill because an AI-assisted surgeon was out-of-network, highlighting opaque billing and admin failures. — [source]
  • Patients frequently discharge against medical advice due to friction in hospital administrative processes and insurance misalignment. — [source]

Problem

The US healthcare system wastes $265 billion annually on administrative complexity. Providers and patients spend millions of hours fighting insurance companies over prior authorizations, resulting in life-threatening delays in care and contributing directly to the financial ruin trap where 40% of low-to-moderate-income workers carry medical debt.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Insurers financially benefit from delaying or denying care by holding onto capital longer.
  • Legacy EHR systems are siloed and require manual human intervention to extract clinical justification.
  • Entrenched lobbying by PBMs and payers prevents legislative simplification of the claims process.

AI-First Solution

ClearAuth AI sits inside the provider's EHR, using fine-tuned Large Language Models to read unstructured physician notes, cross-reference them against specific payer clinical guidelines stored in a vector database, and automatically generate and submit the precise X12/FHIR payload required for instant prior authorization approval.

  • Core: Multi-modal LLM extraction and deterministic mapping to payer-specific policy rules.
  • Auto-Adjudication Engine — reduces manual prior auth review time by 92%.
  • Epic/Cerner SMART on FHIR integration — sits natively in the physician's workflow.
  • Denial Predictive Loop — analyzes past denials to prompt physicians for missing documentation before submission.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Percentage of authorizations approved without human intervention
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital beds contracted
  • Economic KPI: Cost to process per authorization (Target: <$1.50)

2) TriageNet: Autonomous Patient Flow & Virtual Wards — Score: 85/100

ID: c8f921a4-7d6b-4e32-a1f5-9b8c7d6e5f4a

TL;DR: An edge-AI computer vision and continuous risk-scoring platform that eliminates critical wait time deaths in overburdened global ERs.

Evidence

  • UK patients report 9-hour ER waits and up to 2-year waitlists for major surgeries due to systemic understaffing. — [source]
  • China is so overwhelmed that 119 regions have introduced facial recognition just to manage hospital lobby queues. — [source]
  • Nigeria has a doctor-patient ratio of 1:3,500, leading to massive triage failures and a 28.5% global share of maternal deaths. — [source]

Problem

Global healthcare faces an insurmountable access bottleneck. Due to the severe human capital hemorrhage (e.g., Nigeria losing 12k doctors to the UK) and aging populations, physical ERs are overwhelmed. Patients are dying in waiting rooms because static triage (checking vitals once at entry) fails to catch rapid deterioration during 9-hour waits.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • You cannot print doctors; it takes 10-14 years to train specialists, meaning supply will not meet demographic demand.
  • Developing nations cannot compete with Western salaries, ensuring a permanent brain drain.
  • Hospital physical infrastructure is fixed and incredibly capital-intensive to expand.

AI-First Solution

TriageNet uses privacy-preserving edge AI cameras and ambient sensors in waiting rooms and virtual wards to continuously monitor patient vitals (heart rate via rPPG, respiratory rate, mobility). A federated learning model calculates real-time dynamic risk scores (like NEWS2) and alerts the skeletal nursing staff instantly when a patient in the queue begins to deteriorate.

  • Core: Continuous, contactless vital sign extraction via edge computer vision and dynamic risk stratification.
  • Dynamic Queue Reordering — Outcome metric: 0% in-queue mortality.
  • HL7/FHIR EHR Sync — Pushes real-time alerts to existing nurse station dashboards.
  • Virtual Ward Handoff — Allows stable patients to be sent home with mobile-camera monitoring, freeing physical beds.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in adverse events (codes/deaths) in waiting areas
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital waiting rooms/wards deployed
  • Economic KPI: Hardware/Software gross margin (Target: >75%)

3) MediRoute: Predictive Procurement AI — Score: 84/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a11-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d

TL;DR: A predictive supply chain AI that eliminates essential drug stockouts in emerging markets by forecasting localized demand and automatically routing around corrupt or inefficient procurement nodes.

Evidence

  • Mexico's public system ordered 381 million units of medication, but logistical failures resulted in only a 61% delivery rate, causing severe insulin shortages. — [source]
  • Indonesian regional hospitals experience systemic failures where basic resources are not distributed equitably outside major urban centers. — [source]
  • Nigerian healthcare workers cite the total lack of basic medical supplies like gloves and syringes as a primary driver of their emigration. — [source]

Problem

Emerging markets suffer from a fractured medical supply chain. Despite adequate national budgets, hospitals consistently run out of essential drugs (insulin, antibiotics) and basic supplies. This is driven by corrupt state-run procurement processes, lack of localized demand forecasting, and reliance on fragile global API supply chains, directly causing patient deaths and driving medical inflation up to 14.9% in LatAm.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • State-run procurement systems are highly centralized, opaque, and resistant to change due to political patronage and corruption.
  • Hospital IT systems in emerging markets are fragmented, meaning national distributors have zero visibility into real-time local inventory.
  • Geopolitical tensions and climate disruptions make the global flow of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) highly volatile.

AI-First Solution

MediRoute deploys a federated learning network across regional hospital EMRs and pharmacy inventory systems. It uses time-series forecasting to predict localized disease spikes (e.g., a dengue outbreak driving IV fluid demand) and automatically executes smart contracts via blockchain to route procurement directly from secondary regional distributors, bypassing bottlenecked state nodes before stockouts occur.

  • Core: Federated time-series forecasting combined with automated multi-node procurement routing.
  • Predictive Stockout Dashboard — Outcome metric: 90% reduction in critical drug stockouts.
  • GS1 EPCIS Integration — Provides real-time track-and-trace visibility from manufacturer to hospital bed.
  • Dynamic Supplier Switching — Automatically reroutes POs to alternative vetted suppliers if the primary node shows latency.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Critical medication stockout rate (%) at partner hospitals
  • Traction KPI: Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) of medical supplies routed through the platform
  • Economic KPI: Take rate / Transaction fee margin (Target: 3-5%)

## Subject: Daily Early-Diagnosis Brief — MASLD/MASH (Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease / Steatohepatitis) — 2026-06-09

---

### 1) Snapshot (one line)

**MASLD/MASH** — early diagnosis fails in practice because the only definitive test is an invasive liver biopsy, while the non-invasive alternatives used in primary care (routine LFTs) are blind to the majority of patients with silent, significant fibrosis until cirrhosis or HCC is already established.

---

### 2) Why Early Diagnosis Fails (3–5 bullets)

- **Biological barrier:** MASLD progresses silently through steatosis → inflammation (MASH) → fibrosis → cirrhosis over 10–20 years with no symptoms, no jaundice, no pain. ALT/AST — the primary care tripwire — can remain *normal* even at F2–F3 fibrosis stages, creating a false reassurance loop.
- **Test limitation:** Liver biopsy (gold standard) samples only 1/50,000th of the liver, carries bleeding risk (~1 in 1,000), costs $3,000–$8,000 per procedure, and is wholly impractical at the scale of a disease affecting ~38% of the global adult population (~1.2 billion people). FIB-4 — the current primary care workhorse — has known age-dependent bias and generates a high "indeterminate zone" (1.3–2.67), meaning ~40% of patients are unresolved after first-line screening.
- **System failure (primary care):** GPs are trained to use ALT/AST as the liver sentinel. The majority of patients with significant fibrosis (F2+) have normal or minimally elevated LFTs — they are systematically invisible in routine metabolic checkups. There is no standardized reflex testing protocol in primary care when FIB-4 is indeterminate.
- **System failure (referral pathway):** Hepatology services are overwhelmed. Without a validated, affordable, second-line non-invasive test between FIB-4 and biopsy, patients in the indeterminate zone are either over-referred (straining hepatology) or lost to follow-up. No country has yet implemented a national MASLD fibrosis screening pathway.
- **Therapeutic urgency newly unmet:** Resmetirom (Rezdiffra, FDA-approved March 2024 for MASH with fibrosis F2–F3) and semaglutide (approved for MASH) create an urgent commercial and clinical need to *identify eligible patients non-invasively* — a need the diagnostic infrastructure cannot yet serve at scale.

Only 1 in 3 patients with cirrhosis is diagnosed at a stage where reversal or meaningful intervention is possible.

### 4) What's Being Used Today (Gold Standard + Emergent)

**Gold Standards:**
- **Liver biopsy + histology (NASH CRN scoring / SAF score):** Definitive for MASH diagnosis and fibrosis staging. Impractical for screening. Subject to sampling error.
- **FIB-4 index** (platelets × age / AST × √ALT): Recommended by EASL/AASLD as first-line non-invasive triage. Cheap, blood-based. High false-negative rate in young patients; high indeterminate zone.
- **VCTE / FibroScan (Echosens):** Vibration-controlled transient elastography — best validated point-of-care imaging tool. High diagnostic accuracy for fibrosis and steatosis (AUC 0.85–0.93 for F3+). Requires trained operator, affected by obesity/ascites.

**Emerging Research / Tools (2024–2026):**
- **ELF Test (Siemens Healthineers)** — TIMP-1 + PIIINP + hyaluronic acid panel; FDA Breakthrough Device designation for MASLD. Best validated for F3+ risk stratification.
- **M2BPGi (Mac-2 Binding Protein Glycosylation Isomer)** — 2026 data shows sequential FIB-4 + M2BPGi algorithm significantly reduces indeterminate results; robust for F3/cirrhosis detection. Strong candidate for second-line primary care use.
- **PRO-C3 (Nordic Bioscience)** — Type III collagen neo-epitope; specific to *active fibrogenesis* (not static scar). Predicts MASH and fibrosis *progression* — uniquely valuable for treatment monitoring.
- **5-protein plasma proteomics panel** — 2025 study: predicts MASLD onset 16 years in advance at 76% accuracy. Pre-clinical but transformative if validated.
- **Serum miRNA multiplex panels** (miR-122, miR-192, miR-21, miR-34a) — 2025/2026 data shows significantly higher sensitivity/specificity for early MASLD staging vs. traditional serum markers.
- **13C-methionine breath test (MeBT)** — Non-invasive, reflects mitochondrial dysfunction specific to MASH. Clinical validation ongoing (ScienceDirect 2025).
- **Nanose "DiaNose" POC breath test** — Volatile organic compound breathalyzer for rapid liver disease triage; featured at EASL SLD Summit January 2025.
- **PathAI Diagnostics AI-LDT** — World's first AI-assisted Laboratory Developed Test for MASLD/MASH histologic scoring; reduces inter-pathologist variability, makes biopsy interpretation more consistent.
- **"Agile scores" (VCTE + routine labs)** — JAMA 2024: highly predictive of liver-related clinical events; validated as biopsy-alternative for risk stratification.

**Main Limitations:**
- ELF/M2BPGi: Not yet standard-of-care in primary care; reimbursement gaps in most healthcare systems.
- VCTE: Operator-dependent; BMI >35 reduces accuracy; requires dedicated device (~$30K–$80K).
- miRNA/proteomics: Not yet commercially available as clinical assays; require validation in diverse populations.
- Breath tests: Early-stage clinical evidence; not yet CE-marked or FDA-cleared for MASLD.

### 5) Where Healthcare Is Failing (Operational Insight)

**Screening point that drops the ball:**
Primary care metabolic review. The standard-of-care "liver check" is ALT/AST — tests that are *normal in the majority of patients with F2–F3 fibrosis*. The diagnostic journey only begins when patients are symptomatic (ascites, jaundice) — by which point F4 cirrhosis or HCC is often established. The FIB-4 score, though recommended by EASL/AASLD, is *not routinely calculated* in most GP EMR systems — it requires manual entry of 3 values (age, platelets, ALT, AST) that are rarely co-ordered.

**Bottleneck most fixable in 90 days:**
**Automated FIB-4 calculation + reflex M2BPGi/ELF flagging in the EMR.** In any hospital or health system with an integrated lab-EMR interface: (a) auto-calculate FIB-4 whenever a metabolic panel is ordered in patients with T2DM, obesity, or hypertension; (b) auto-flag indeterminate results (FIB-4 1.3–2.67) for reflex ELF or M2BPGi add-on without requiring a separate GP order. This is a *software + protocol change*, not a new device purchase. Estimated cost: near-zero if lab already runs ELF.

**High-risk population missed:**
- **T2DM patients in primary care:** ~70% of T2DM patients have MASLD; ~20% have significant fibrosis. They are seen regularly but liver fibrosis is not on the standard diabetic review checklist.
- **Patients with metabolic syndrome + normal LFTs:** The "normal LFT" falsely reassures GPs. This group has the highest proportion of silent F2–F3 fibrosis.
- **South/East Asian populations:** Lower BMI thresholds for MASLD; current FIB-4 age-cutoffs are calibrated on Western cohorts and underperform in Asian patients.

### 7) First-Principles Signal Hunt (What We Should Measure Earlier)

**Hidden signal candidate:**
The earliest measurable signal of MASH is not hepatocyte damage (ALT) but *hepatic mitochondrial dysfunction* and *active fibrogenesis*. Two underutilized proxies:
1. **PRO-C3** — neo-epitope of type III collagen released during active scar formation; rises *before* fibrosis is histologically visible. Currently used in clinical trials but almost never in routine care.
2. **Circulating miR-122** — the most liver-specific miRNA; released from hepatocytes during lipotoxic stress, *before* ALT elevation. Detectable in serum at steatosis stage.
3. **Breath VOC signature** — exhaled isoprene, acetone, and pentanal patterns reflect hepatic oxidative stress and fat metabolism; measurable non-invasively, no blood draw required. Nanose/DiaNose is the leading POC device in this space.

**Minimal sampling change needed:**
- Add PRO-C3 and miR-122 to an existing fasting metabolic blood panel (same blood draw, no additional patient contact).
- For breath: a 30-second breath collection into a sealed tube at clinic check-in — zero additional workflow burden.

### 10) Pattern Insight (Meta-Learning)

**Recurring diagnostic failure pattern emerging:**

This is the **"Normal Sentinel Trap"** — a pattern now seen across multiple briefs: the test used as the primary clinical tripwire is *biologically incapable of detecting the disease in its actionable window*, yet it persists in practice because it is cheap, familiar, and embedded in guidelines written before better alternatives existed.

| Disease | False Sentinel | What It Misses |
|---|---|---|
| MASLD/MASH | ALT/AST | F2–F3 fibrosis with normal LFTs |
| CKD | Serum creatinine | GFR decline until >50% nephron loss |
| Pancreatic Cancer | CA19-9 alone | Early-stage PDAC (low specificity) |
| Heart Failure | Symptomatic presentation | Pre-HF BNP elevation in asymptomatic patients |
| Autoimmune (SLE) | ANA titer | Prodromal phase before ANA positivity |

**Is today's disease reinforcing or breaking the pattern?**
**Reinforcing strongly.** MASLD is the canonical example of the Normal Sentinel Trap at population scale — 1.2 billion people at risk, screened with a test (ALT) that misses the majority of significant pathology.

**Generalizable opportunity forming:**
The cross-disease opportunity is a **"Process Marker Layer"** — a second tier of blood-based or breath-based markers that measure *active biological processes* (fibrogenesis, mitochondrial stress, inflammatory cascades) rather than *end-organ damage*. PRO-C3 (collagen synthesis), miR-122 (hepatocyte stress), breath VOCs (oxidative metabolism), and plasma proteomics are all examples of this layer for MASLD. The same architectural shift — from damage markers to process markers, embedded in routine care workflows — is the generalizable innovation opportunity across CKD (UACR as process marker vs. creatinine), HF (BNP/NT-proBNP as process marker vs. symptoms), and neurodegeneration (p-tau181/GFAP as process markers vs. cognitive decline). **The startup/research white space: a universal "process marker" multiplexed panel, ordered at metabolic risk assessment, that flags biological fires before the damage alarm sounds.**

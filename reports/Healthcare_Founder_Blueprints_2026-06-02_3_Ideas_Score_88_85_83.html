1) Aegis Health: Generative AI for Prior Auth & Denial Reversal — Score: 88/100

ID: e4b9c1a5-8f2d-4b9e-a1c2-3d4f5e6a7b8c

TL;DR: An AI-agentic platform that auto-generates clinical appeals directly from EMRs, turning administrative violence into a solved $100B engineering problem.

Evidence

  • Patients accumulate over $1 million in synthetic medical debt from blanket insurance denials. — [source]
  • Healthcare violence directly coincides with billing discussions and insurance denials. — [source]
  • $220 billion in medical debt is currently held by over 100 million people in the US. — [source]

Problem

Complex insurance bureaucracies actively deny necessary care to protect profit margins, creating an administrative nightmare. This leads to physical hospital violence, severe provider burnout, and patients facing catastrophic $1M+ synthetic debts. Millions of claims are denied annually, requiring a massive, hidden workforce just to fight administrative battles between hospitals and insurers.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Profit-driven insurance models financially incentivize denying or delaying care.
  • Regulatory capture by the insurance industry prevents meaningful simplification or standardization.
  • Administrative bloat is a feature, not a bug, for insurers to wear down providers.

AI-First Solution

An LLM-powered agent that ingests FHIR patient data and payer guidelines to auto-draft bulletproof prior authorizations and instantly counter denials with clinically cited appeal letters.

  • Core: RAG over payer-specific medical policies and EMR clinical notes for automated reasoning.
  • First-pass denial overturn rate tracking and optimization.
  • EPIC/Cerner SMART on FHIR integration for seamless biller workflow.
  • Continuous learning loop from successful vs. failed appeals to update prompt strategies.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Denial Overturn Rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Hospital systems signed
  • Economic KPI: Net Revenue Retained (NRR) / Gross Margin

2) TriageOS: Predictive Waitlist Management — Score: 85/100

ID: f5c0d2b6-903e-5c0f-b2d3-4e5f6a7b8c9d

TL;DR: An AI triage engine that dynamically re-prioritizes public health waitlists, preventing critical patient deterioration while solving the $200B backlog crisis.

Evidence

  • NHS wait times have become a systemic failure with patients facing massive backlogs for specialties. — [source]
  • Brazil's SUS forces citizens to rely heavily on out-of-pocket spending for timely care due to systemic inefficiencies. — [source]
  • Normal wait times stretching to 10+ hours at local clinics and hospitals due to overwhelmed infrastructure. — [source]

Problem

Universal systems like the NHS and Brazil's SUS are buckling under demand. Wait times for non-emergency procedures stretch well over a year, forcing a two-tier system where patients deteriorate while waiting, leading to catastrophic downstream emergency costs and overwhelmed ERs.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Chronic underfunding relative to population growth limits physical capacity.
  • Inefficient legacy IT infrastructure relies on static, chronological lists rather than clinical urgency.
  • Severe workforce shortages limit throughput and manual triage capacity.

AI-First Solution

An NLP-driven clinical prioritization engine that reads referral letters, EMR updates, and patient-reported symptoms to dynamically risk-stratify waitlists, automatically escalating hidden critical cases.

  • Core: Temporal clinical NLP over longitudinal patient records to predict deterioration.
  • Dynamic risk score & escalation alert for triage nurses.
  • NHS e-Referral Service (e-RS) / SUS integration for automated queue updates.
  • Outcome-based threshold tuning based on actual ER admissions.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Reduction in wait-time adverse events (%)
  • Traction KPI: Hospital Trusts / Municipalities deployed
  • Economic KPI: Annual Contract Value (ACV) per Trust

3) Nightingale Node: Edge AI for Clinical Task-Shifting — Score: 83/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-4a5b-8c9d-0e1f2a3b4c5d

TL;DR: An offline-first, AI clinical decision support system empowering nurses in brain-drain affected regions to safely diagnose and manage chronic diseases.

Evidence

  • Nigeria lost 9,000 doctors to the UK, US, and Canada between 2016 and 2018; 65% are employed overseas. — [source]
  • Nigeria's doctor-to-patient ratio has plummeted to a devastating 1 to 9,000 against WHO recommendations. — [source]
  • Singapore and GCC face severe healthcare crises driven by aging populations, turning to connected care and cost discipline. — [source]

Problem

Massive global wage disparities have caused a one-directional exodus of doctors from developing nations to wealthy aging nations. This leaves origin systems with a 1:9,000 doctor-to-patient ratio, completely unable to manage the rising tide of chronic diseases and basic care without task-shifting to nurses.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Wealthy nations will not stop recruiting due to their own aging population crises.
  • Origin countries cannot compete economically to retain their top medical talent.
  • Training new doctors takes 10+ years, offering no short-term relief.

AI-First Solution

An offline-capable tablet application running quantized LLMs and diagnostic models locally, guiding Community Health Workers (CHWs) and nurses through standardized diagnostic pathways to safely task-shift physician work.

  • Core: Edge-quantized multimodal diagnostic AI that operates without internet connectivity.
  • Diagnostic accuracy tracking vs remote physician baseline.
  • Asynchronous sync to national DHIS2 databases upon internet reconnection.
  • Local epidemiological anomaly detection to flag outbreaks.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Patient encounters successfully managed by CHWs (without MD escalation)
  • Traction KPI: NGO / Government clinics deployed
  • Economic KPI: Cost per patient encounter

12-Week MVP

WeeksDeliverableSuccess Metric
W1-W4Quantize medical LLM for edge device<2s latency on mobile CPU
W5-W8Build offline Android app with DHIS2 sync100% data sync success on reconnect
W9-W12Field test with 10 CHWs in rural clinic80%+ clinical concordance with remote MD

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: Android (Kotlin), Llama.cpp, SQLite, TensorFlow Lite
  • Datasets: WHO clinical guidelines, local formulary data, symptom-disease matrices
  • Integrations: DHIS2 (District Health Information Software), OpenMRS

Regulatory Path

  • NAFDAC (Nigeria) or PPB (Kenya) medical device guidelines for diagnostic software.
  • WHO Digital Health clearance for global NGO procurement.
  • Local Ministry of Health data sovereignty and patient consent compliance.

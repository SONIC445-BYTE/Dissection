1) Aegis RCM: Autonomous Claim Defense Engine — Score: 88/100

ID: e4b3b2a1-7c9d-4e5f-8a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b

TL;DR: An LLM-powered Revenue Cycle Management agent that auto-generates mathematically airtight insurance appeals, unlocking a $120B+ TAM by reversing predatory claim denials.

Evidence

  • The net cost of private health insurance administration in the US is over $306 billion annually. — [source]
  • AHPI threatened to suspend cashless services for Star Health Insurance due to arbitrary claim rejections and 13,300 complaints. — [source]
  • US physicians and patients rank worst globally in facing hurdles related to insurance rules and billing disputes. — [source]

Problem

Hospitals and providers face massive, intentional bureaucratic bloat designed by insurers to delay care and deny claims. This administrative hostility wears down providers, leading to billions in abandoned revenue and severely delayed patient care, as seen with Star Health's 13,300 complaints in India and the US's $306B administrative burden.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • The core business model of private insurance relies on the 'float' and maximizing denial rates; complexity is a profitable feature.
  • A massive sub-economy of billing coders and claims adjusters relies on this friction, resisting automation.
  • Payer rules are highly fragmented, constantly changing, and buried in thousands of pages of unstructured policy PDFs.

AI-First Solution

Aegis RCM deploys agentic AI to autonomously cross-reference EMR clinical notes against constantly updating payer policy manuals. It predicts denial risks before claims are submitted and automatically drafts comprehensive, citation-backed appeal letters for rejected claims.

  • Core: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over unstructured payer policy manuals and EMR clinical notes.
  • Pre-Submission Denial Predictor — Flagging claims with >80% denial probability before submission.
  • Auto-Appeal Generator — One-click generation of 5-page appeal letters citing specific medical necessity guidelines.
  • Policy Drift Detector — Continuous scraping of payer portals to update the vector database when authorization rules change.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Initial Claim Denial Rate (%)
  • Traction KPI: Number of hospital networks deployed
  • Economic KPI: Net revenue recovered per hospital ($)

W9-W12Live pilot run with a mid-sized clinic processing 1,000 live claims.Overturn 20% of previously 'dead' denied claims, generating immediate ROI.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: vLLM for fast inference, Pinecone Vector DB, LangChain for agentic workflows.
  • Datasets: Historical 835/837 EDI claim files, unstructured payer policy PDFs, EMR clinical notes.
  • Integrations: Epic/Cerner FHIR endpoints, Clearinghouse APIs (e.g., Change Healthcare/Waystar).

Regulatory Path

  • Not a medical device (Administrative Software); no FDA/CDSCO pre-market approval required.
  • HIPAA (US) / DPDP Act (India) compliance requiring BAA execution and zero-retention LLM APIs.
  • SOC2 Type II certification required for enterprise hospital procurement.

  • Deep integration into hospital EMR and clearinghouse workflows creates extreme switching costs.
  • Network effects: A successful appeal strategy in one hospital instantly updates the model for all others.

2) PathwayZero: Asynchronous Triage & Borderless Routing — Score: 85/100

ID: f5c4c3b2-8d0e-5f60-9b2c-3d4e5f6a7b8c

TL;DR: A multimodal AI triage agent that safely resolves low-acuity cases and dynamically routes high-acuity patients to vetted cross-border clinics, attacking a $150B+ access bottleneck.

Evidence

  • NHS waitlists routinely exceed 7 million incomplete pathways, leaving patients in prolonged pain. — [source]
  • Canadian wait times have become so severe that patients are crossing the border to the US for urgent care. — [source]
  • Patients are forced to endure prolonged pain or pay out-of-pocket for private care due to months-long waits. — [source]

Problem

Universal healthcare systems like the UK's NHS and Canada's Medicare are buckling under chronic underfunding and workforce shortages. Millions of patients are trapped on waitlists exceeding 7 months for basic specialist care, leading to severe operational bottlenecks and forcing desperate patients into unmanaged medical tourism.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • It takes 10 to 15 years to train a specialist physician; the human capital required to clear the backlog simply does not exist.
  • Public systems are constrained by rigid geographic and bureaucratic boundaries, unable to dynamically load-balance across regions.
  • Legacy triage protocols are highly manual, requiring expensive nursing staff to perform basic intake and prioritization.

AI-First Solution

PathwayZero is an asynchronous, AI-driven triage and routing network. It uses clinically fine-tuned LLMs to conduct deep patient intake, autonomously resolving or deferring low-acuity cases. For high-acuity cases stuck in 6+ month public waitlists, the AI matches the patient with vetted, available capacity in private networks or cross-border clinics (e.g., Spain, UAE, US), handling predictive cost and outcome modeling.

  • Core: Multimodal clinical NLP for safe triage combined with dynamic global capacity matching algorithms.
  • Asynchronous Intake Agent — Conducts deep, adaptive patient interviews via text/voice to build a structured clinical summary.
  • Waitlist Bypass Router — API integration with private clinic scheduling systems to offer immediate alternative care options.
  • Outcome & Safety Monitor — Automated follow-up loops to ensure diverted patients received care and to train the triage safety model.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Days removed from patient wait time
  • Traction KPI: Number of patients successfully routed to alternative care
  • Economic KPI: Cost per acquisition (CPA) vs Routing Commission

12-Week MVP

WeeksDeliverableSuccess Metric
W1-W4Clinical safety fine-tuning of Llama-3 for top 5 elective surgery intakes (e.g., orthopedics, cataracts).99% concordance with human triage nurses on 1,000 historical patient vignettes.
W5-W8Provider portal and capacity scraping tool for 10 partner private clinics.Real-time availability syncing with <1 hour latency.
W9-W12DTC pilot launch in the UK targeting patients waiting >3 months for knee/hip replacements.Successfully route and facilitate 50 cross-border or private consultations.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: Fine-tuned Llama-3 (Clinical), Whisper (Voice), Postgres (Routing Logic), Next.js.
  • Datasets: NHS/Canadian triage guidelines, private clinic pricing/availability data, historical patient pathways.
  • Integrations: Stripe Connect (payments), Private Clinic EMR scheduling APIs, Twilio (Voice/SMS).

Regulatory Path

  • UK MHRA Class IIa Medical Device certification required for AI-driven clinical triage.
  • Strict GDPR compliance for processing special category health data.
  • Cross-border legal framework establishment for medical liability and patient transfer.

3) EquiBill AI: Medical Bill Auditing & Price Enforcement — Score: 83/100

ID: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7a8b-9c0d-1e2f3a4b5c6d

TL;DR: An AI financial advocate that ingests itemized hospital bills to detect predatory markups and auto-negotiates using regulatory price transparency data, capturing a $15B+ market.

Evidence

  • A private hospital in Mumbai refused to release a deceased patient's body until the family paid an additional ₹40 lakhs. — [source]
  • 41% of Americans spent $1,000 or more out-of-pocket in 2024, leading to severe medical debt. — [source]
  • Healthcare enforcement and litigation are rising sharply as patients battle complex insurance coverage denials. — [source]

Problem

Unchecked healthcare corporatization has led to predatory billing practices. Patients are routinely subjected to grossly inflated charges (e.g., 400% markups on basic medicines), opaque pricing, and hostage-like negotiation tactics, resulting in crippling medical debt and avoidance of necessary care.

Why It Will Remain Unsolved

  • Regulatory frameworks (like India's CEA or US Price Transparency rules) exist but lack automated enforcement mechanisms.
  • Hospital billing systems are intentionally opaque, outputting hundreds of cryptic line items that consumers cannot decipher.
  • The political class often has financial ties to private hospital lobbies, stalling top-down legislative fixes.

AI-First Solution

EquiBill AI empowers patients and self-insured employers with a computer-vision and LLM-powered auditing agent. Users upload photos or PDFs of their hospital bills. The AI extracts every line item, cross-references it against state-mandated pricing benchmarks (e.g., CEA rates, Medicare reference pricing), flags predatory markups, and auto-generates legal dispute letters to force the hospital to reduce the bill.

  • Core: Multimodal OCR and entity extraction combined with a massive proprietary database of regional fair-market healthcare prices.
  • Line-Item Markup Detector — Instantly highlights specific drugs or procedures billed at >200% of fair market value.
  • Auto-Dispute Generator — Drafts legal-grade emails to hospital billing departments citing specific price transparency laws.
  • Crowdsourced Price Index — Feeds every uploaded bill into a central database to build real-time pricing benchmarks for specific hospitals.

Key Metrics

  • North Star: Total Dollars Saved for Patients ($)
  • Traction KPI: Number of bills uploaded & processed weekly
  • Economic KPI: Conversion rate to paid/premium negotiation tier

12-Week MVP

WeeksDeliverableSuccess Metric
W1-W4OCR pipeline and LLM extraction prompt tuned for complex, multi-page hospital bills.95% accuracy in extracting CPT codes, descriptions, and prices from 200 sample bills.
W5-W8Pricing rule engine integrating US CMS transparency data and Indian CEA benchmark data.Successfully flag at least 1 predatory line item in 80% of processed bills.
W9-W12Consumer web app launch allowing users to upload bills and download a free dispute letter.1,000 bills processed with an average identified savings of $500+ per bill.

Tech & Data Needs

  • Core Stack: AWS Textract / GCP Document AI, GPT-4o for complex reasoning, PostgreSQL for price indexing.
  • Datasets: US Hospital Price Transparency machine-readable files, Indian State CEA rate cards, historical billing data.
  • Integrations: Plaid (for verifying medical debt payments), SendGrid (for auto-emailing hospital billing depts).

Regulatory Path

  • Not a medical device; purely financial and consumer advocacy software.
  • Requires strict adherence to CCPA (US) and DPDP Act (India) regarding consumer financial and health data.
  • Must include legal disclaimers that the AI is providing financial advocacy, not formal legal representation.

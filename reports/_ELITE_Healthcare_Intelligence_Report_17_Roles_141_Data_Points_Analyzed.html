6. HOSPITAL DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

  • Hardened Triage Barriers: Front desk and emergency triage areas must be physically redesigned with shatter-proof glass and secure access doors to protect staff from immediate mob violence.
  • Restorative Safe Zones: Doctors and nurses require secure, unbreachable break rooms to combat the 91% burnout rate. These must be completely inaccessible to patients and families.
  • Automated Billing Kiosks: Remove the human element from the initial shock of rejected claims. Let patients interface with a machine that explains TPA rejections before they speak to a human.
  • Secure Diagnostic Enclaves: Radiology and pathology wings must have strict biometric access to prevent privacy breaches (e.g., unauthorized filming).

## What this is

The single document that makes every prior phase actionable. If someone reads only one artefact from this knowledge base, it is this one — and it must be **traceable back to evidence at every claim**.

## Context
✅ Everything. All phases, all dossiers, all reports, repo, dissection, blueprint.

## Deliverable: `JARVIS-MASTER-STRATEGY.md`

### 1. Executive summary
One page. What JARVIS is, what it is becoming, why it wins, what could kill it, what happens next.

Written last. Written for someone who will read nothing else.

### 2. The strategic case
Grounded in corpus evidence:
- What the ecosystem actually looks like (Phase 6)
- Where value is genuinely available (Phase 3, 6)
- What JARVIS can uniquely do (Phase 7, 8)
- **Why now** — and what would make "now" wrong

### 3. Engineering roadmap
Stage-honest. Every item traceable to a finding. Every item with a stop condition.

### 4. Architecture
Target architecture with the evidence that forces each decision. Include what is **deliberately not** built.

### 5. Healthcare roadmap
Workflows, standards, adapters — in the order Phase 5's real integration costs justify. India-first, tier-honest.

### 6. Platform roadmap
SDK, plugins, marketplace, community — **only if the evidence supports it.** Premature platform plays are a documented failure mode.

### 7. Competitive roadmap
Per Strategic Role, not per company:
- **Foundational Dependencies** → mitigation and second-sourcing
- **Integration Targets** → adapter order and effort
- **Direct Competitors** → differentiation (structural, not feature-level)
- **Potential Partners** → named joint surfaces
- **Technology Suppliers** → build-vs-buy decisions
- **Market Signals** → watch triggers

### 8. Research roadmap
Open questions worth investigating. Explicitly include **research that may fail** — with falsifiers, and with a rule that it never blocks delivery.

### 9. Investment roadmap
Where effort goes, in what ratio. Include the honest ratio of *mission-critical* to *engineering-satisfying* work — Phase R found that ratio skewed, and it is the kind of thing that only stays fixed if it is measured.

### 10. Category roadmap
What category does JARVIS create or occupy? Category creation is expensive; category entry is crowded. Choose deliberately and state the cost.

### 11. Ten-year vision
Where this goes if it works. Kept short and unsentimental.

### 12. ⭐ Decision Ledger
Every consequential decision, with:

| ID | Decision | Date | Rationale | Evidence | **Reversal trigger** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**The reversal trigger is the most important column.** A decision without a pre-agreed condition for revisiting it becomes an assumption, and assumptions calcify. Write the trigger *before* it fires, because it will not feel like the right time to change course when it does.

### 13. ⭐ Unknown unknowns
What is *not* known, and what is the plan for finding out?

Include:
- Questions the corpus could not answer
- Areas where evidence was thin
- Assumptions that remain untested
- **What would have to be true for this whole strategy to be wrong**

> Most strategy documents project certainty. This section is where the document earns trust: by being explicit about the edges of its own knowledge. A strategy that cannot name its own failure conditions has not been thought through.

## Discipline

- **Every claim traceable.** Untraceable assertions are deleted, not softened.
- **Stage-honest.** No roadmap items that assume capabilities that don't exist.
- **Reversal triggers mandatory** for every consequential decision.
- **Uncomfortable findings preserved.** They do not get sanded off in final synthesis — that is exactly where the sanding usually happens.
- **The validation question answered.** If it still isn't, the strategy is not ready.

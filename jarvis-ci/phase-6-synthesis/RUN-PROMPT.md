## The moment the pipeline was built for

Everything until now was deliberately fragmented — one company per run, no comparison, no repo context. That fragmentation was the price of rigour. **Phase 6 is where it pays off**, because the patterns found here were not available to any individual run and could not have been manufactured by one.

## Context

✅ **Everything** — all Phase 2 dossiers, all Phase 3 layer reports, all Phase 4 technology reports, all Phase 5 healthcare reports, the contradiction ledger
❌ **JARVIS repo / dissection / blueprint** — still forbidden

> The final exclusion is deliberate and it is the last line of defence. Patterns across companies must be found **on their own terms**. A pattern like *"nobody solves memory consolidation"* is either true of the world or it isn't. If it was derived while looking at JARVIS's memory module, it is unfalsifiable and worthless. Phase 7 is where repo reality meets these findings — not before.

## Deliverable: `CROSS-COMPANY-SYNTHESIS.md`

### 1. Shared beliefs
What do **all** these companies believe? Assumptions so universal nobody states them.

> Universal assumptions are the most dangerous kind. They are invisible from inside the industry and they are where category-defining opportunities hide, because everyone has stopped questioning them.

### 2. Divergent beliefs
Where do they genuinely disagree? Each disagreement is a live bet with an eventual winner. Name the bet, the camps, and what evidence would settle it.

### 3. Convergent architecture
What is everyone converging on? **Convergence = commoditisation.** Anything converged is no longer a differentiator, whatever the marketing says.

### 4. Disappearing architecture
What is being abandoned? Deprecations and quiet removals are the most honest signal in the corpus.

### 5. Emerging standards
Which standards are consolidating, and what did they erase? Track the pattern: a standards wave can wipe out a whole class of differentiation in months.

### 6. ⭐ What nobody is solving
Aggregate the negative space from every layer and technology report. For each gap:

| Gap | Confirmed unclaimed? | Why unclaimed — hard, or neglected? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

> **The hard/neglected distinction is the entire value of this section.** A gap that is unclaimed because it is *neglected* is an opportunity. A gap unclaimed because it is *genuinely hard* is a trap that has already consumed better-funded teams. Confusing the two is how startups die confidently.

### 7. ⭐ What everyone is overbuilding
Where is the industry over-investing? Crowded categories with converged architecture are where marginal effort earns least.

### 8. Failure patterns
Aggregate every Phase 2 failure analysis, especially the post-mortems (Olive AI, Forward Health, Pear Therapeutics). What kills companies in this ecosystem, ranked by frequency?

### 9. Moat patterns
Which moats actually held? Which were claimed but evaporated? Distinguish *durable* from *rented* — distribution deals and platform placement are rented moats that read like owned ones.

### 10. Value migration
Where is value moving between layers? Test the taxonomy's claim (`01-canonical-taxonomy.md` §3.2) that value migrates up as lower layers commoditise, **but distribution can capture from any layer at any time**.

### 11. The Agent Core hypothesis — test it properly
`01-canonical-taxonomy.md` §3.1 claims whoever owns the L3–L6 loop's persistent state owns the user.

**Look for disconfirming evidence specifically:**
- Cases where a single-box owner captured the loop anyway
- Cases where a loop-owner was disintermediated from above (L8) or below (L1)
- Whether loop ownership actually produced durable advantage, or just the appearance of it

Report the verdict honestly, including "the hypothesis is wrong."

### 12. Contradiction resolution
Every remaining cross-dossier conflict, resolved with reasoning or declared irresolvable.

---

## Method discipline

- **Patterns need ≥3 instances.** Two is a coincidence.
- **Cite dossier claim IDs.** A synthesis claim with no traceable evidence is an opinion.
- **Actively seek disconfirmation.** For every pattern, ask what would falsify it and check whether that exists in the corpus.
- **Report absent patterns.** "We expected X and found no evidence" is a genuine result.
- **Do not smooth contradictions.** An unresolved conflict reported honestly beats a false consensus.
